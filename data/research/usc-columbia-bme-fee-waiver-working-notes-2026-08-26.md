# University of South Carolina Biomedical Engineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

The current USC Graduate School admissions page states that the US$50 graduate application fee was reinstated effective August 1, 2026 and that the Graduate School does not grant individual student requests for application-fee waivers. The official Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. degree page identifies the Ph.D. as an Engineering and Computing graduate program using the general Graduate School application. The official Biomedical Engineering M.Eng. page likewise identifies the M.Eng. under Engineering and Computing and uses the general application.

No Biomedical Engineering-specific waiver code, form, event, or exception is published on the current degree pages. The existing paid-fee/no-waiver treatment is retained for the published BME records; no policy from unrelated USC departments is transferred.
