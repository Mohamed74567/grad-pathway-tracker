The BME-specific M.S. and Ph.D. pages support **GRE not required**, optional GRE submission, and annual December 1 Fall guidance with qualified financial-aid/assistantship consideration. Neither reviewed current BME page specifies a Duolingo policy. The current Graduate School page reviewed does not state a fee amount. Any existing fee or Duolingo card must therefore be retained only if independently supported by an active official source.
