# Rochester Institute of Technology Biomedical Engineering MS fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

RIT’s official Graduate How to Apply page states that the standard graduate application fee is US$65 and non-refundable, and that current RIT students and RIT alumni are not required to pay it; the fee is waived within 24 hours after application submission. The official Biomedical Engineering MS page confirms the in-scope degree, its online graduate application, and current admissions requirements. RIT’s official Graduate Admission Deadlines and Requirements page is the current central requirements source and was cross-checked for the MS admissions context.

A degree-specific code, form, hardship route, or admissions-session waiver was not found. The guidance added is therefore a fee-waiver-code/contact-neutral policy statement for current RIT students and alumni only; it is not transferred to an RIT Ph.D. profile. No automatic waiver is claimed for other applicants, and the US$65 fee remains non-refundable for applicants who are not in the published exemption.

Sources: https://www.rit.edu/admissions/graduate/application-instructions ; https://www.rit.edu/study/biomedical-engineering-ms ; https://www.rit.edu/admissions/graduate-admission-deadlines-and-requirements
