## Source-safety implications

The current BME catalog supports a **GRE required for unconditional admission** fact and department-level TOEFL 79 treatment. Central graduate policy supports Duolingo 100, but the department catalog does not name it; it should be used only with explicit central-policy scoping if retained. The current pages reviewed do not publish an exact graduate-application fee, degree-specific deadlines, or a BME Ph.D. funding commitment; those fields should remain blank or not stated unless separately verified from an active official source.
