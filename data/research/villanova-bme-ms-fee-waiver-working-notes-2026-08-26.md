# Villanova Biomedical Engineering M.S. fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

Villanova College of Engineering’s current M.S. application-requirements page states that the College removed the US$50 application fee and that there is no longer an application fee for any College of Engineering graduate program. The official Biomedical Engineering M.S. page confirms that the published program is a College of Engineering graduate degree. No waiver code, form, contact route, or session is needed when no application fee is charged.

The current official College of Engineering Ph.D. application-requirements page carries the same no-application-fee statement, but the current directory cohort selected the published Biomedical Engineering M.S. profile. Existing fee treatment remains unchanged and no fee-waiver guidance is added.
