

## McNair exception follow-up
The current Graduate School FAQ states that although the application fee will not generally be waived, deferred, or refunded, a McNair scholar may receive a waiver; it also notes a department may choose to pay a student’s fee. The linked current McNair Scholars page provides Graduate School contact information but no standalone code or form. Therefore, if represented in the app, use a narrow `fee_waiver_contact` route to gradschool@montana.edu for McNair scholars only, with no guarantee and no claim that ordinary financial hardship qualifies. Do not add the department-payment possibility as an applicant waiver route because it is discretionary and not a published applicant process.
