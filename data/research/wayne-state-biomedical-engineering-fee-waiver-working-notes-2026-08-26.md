# Wayne State Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Confirmed current engineering and master’s evidence

Wayne State’s James and Patricia Anderson College of Engineering graduate-admissions page states that its US$50 application fee is waived for spring, summer, and fall 2026 and winter 2027. The page describes master’s and doctoral engineering programs across the college.

The current Department of Biomedical Engineering M.S. page independently states that the application fee is waived for applicants applying for fall 2026 and winter 2027 terms. This direct M.S. statement supports the existing master’s-specific no-fee treatment for those named terms; it does not by itself prove doctoral applicability or extend to future cycles.

The next review must locate a current Biomedical Engineering doctoral source or verify that the College of Engineering’s engineering-wide wording explicitly governs the named Ph.D. profile before adding shared doctoral guidance.

Sources: https://engineering.wayne.edu/admissions/graduate ; https://engineering.wayne.edu/biomedical/academics/master/bme

## Confirmed shared departmental scope

Wayne State’s current Biomedical Engineering department homepage explicitly announces that application fees are waived for **graduate programs through winter 2027**. This confirms that the College of Engineering’s Spring/Summer/Fall 2026 and Winter 2027 waiver window reaches both published Biomedical Engineering paths, including the Ph.D.; it is not a master’s-only policy. Neither the college nor department page provides a code, form, contact request, approval process, or future-cycle promise. The product therefore displays the bounded fee window directly on both degree paths without adding a speculative fee-waiver-help panel.

## Product validation

On August 26, 2026, loaded doctoral and master’s profile captures both displayed: “US$50 application fee waived for Spring/Summer/Fall 2026 and Winter 2027 applicants.” Neither profile rendered an unnecessary fee-waiver help panel.
