# Florida Atlantic University M.S. in Biomedical Engineering — Working Official-Source Notes

**Review date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** Distinct in-scope master’s degree confirmed. Fee, image, and final publication decision pending.

## Degree and admissions evidence

FAU’s Graduate College identifies a distinct **M.S. in Biomedical Engineering** in the College of Engineering and Computer Science. Its current program page publishes Fall July 1, Spring November 1, and Summer April 1 application deadlines, a 3.0 undergraduate GPA, eligible undergraduate fields, and international requirements of TOEFL 4.5 on the revised scale, IELTS 6.0, or DET 110. The current department page describes the professional and doctoral-preparation outcomes and broad elective selection.

FAU’s current Biomedical Engineering admissions page separately states that GRE is not required, a 3.0 GPA in science/mathematics/engineering courses is expected, and TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.0 is accepted. The Graduate College program list independently labels Biomedical Engineering as GRE/GMAT not required. FAU’s current EngineeringCAS page establishes the direct application route but does not publish a BME-M.S.-applicable application-fee amount.

## Funding and waiver boundary

The current BME assistantship page offers competitive teaching assistantships to eligible BME graduate students with a 3.4 GPA, with priority for doctoral/direct-path/joint BS/MS students, support of up to 20 hours weekly, and proportionate tuition coverage. It does not guarantee support to M.S. applicants. Current FAU sources reviewed so far publish no BME M.S.-specific application-fee amount or application-fee waiver route; these fields stay blank absent a program-applicable source.
