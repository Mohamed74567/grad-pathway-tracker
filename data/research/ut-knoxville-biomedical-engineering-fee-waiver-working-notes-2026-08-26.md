# UT Knoxville Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Confirmed degree boundary

The current UT Knoxville Biomedical Engineering graduate-admission-requirements page states that the department provides application-fee waivers only to **first-time Ph.D. applicants** who are U.S. citizens or U.S. permanent residents. Eligible applicants must contact the department to obtain a code. Students already enrolled at UT who seek a Change of Program are explicitly ineligible.

The page does not state an application-fee waiver for the BME M.S. This direct doctoral-only language prevents transferring the existing Ph.D. route to the separately published M.S. profile. No M.S. code, form, session, contact route, timing, or approval process is published.

Source: https://tickle.utk.edu/bme/academics/graduate/admission-requirements/
