# University of Arkansas Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Current program and central-admissions evidence

The University of Arkansas College of Engineering’s current Biomedical Engineering graduate page confirms the published M.S. in Biomedical Engineering and Ph.D. in Engineering with Biomedical Engineering emphasis, but does not publish a BME-specific application-fee waiver, code, form, session, or process.

The current Graduate School admissions page describes new degree-seeking applicants as paying a US$60 nonrefundable application fee. Its new-degree workflow says an application review begins once the fee and all required documents have been submitted. The central page lists a separate fee-waiver FAQ, which requires direct inspection before any conclusion; neither program source supports transferring a general or returning-student exception to new BME M.S. or Ph.D. applicants.

Sources: https://engineering.uark.edu/biomedical-engineering/graduate.php ; https://graduate-students.uark.edu/admissions/

## Confirmed central-fee-waiver boundary

The expanded current Graduate School fee-waiver FAQ limits waivers to full-time employees at the University of Arkansas main campus, U of A summer REU program participants, and Arkansas-resident senior citizens age 60 or older. These categories do not identify a general new Biomedical Engineering M.S. or Ph.D. applicant route, and the BME page publishes no separate exception. New degree-seeking applicants therefore retain the US$60 nonrefundable fee without a fee-waiver panel.
