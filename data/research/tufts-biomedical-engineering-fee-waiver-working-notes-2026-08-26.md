# Tufts Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Confirmed central policy and M.S. scope

Tufts Graduate School of Arts and Sciences and School of Engineering currently publish fee-waiver benefits for applicants to School of Engineering programs. The official policy lists current/former AmeriCorps volunteers, Leadership Alliance participants, McNair Scholars, current/former Peace Corps volunteers, Teach for America volunteers/alumni, and currently serving or honorably discharged U.S. Armed Forces members. It also describes NESCAC eligibility as Graduate School of Arts and Sciences only, so that category must not be applied to Biomedical Engineering.

For AmeriCorps, Peace Corps, and Teach for America, applicants select the applicable status in the online-application information section and email an official organization letter verifying service years to `gradadmissions@tufts.edu`. Leadership Alliance and McNair applicants likewise select their status and email a verification letter. Military applicants select their status in the application; no separate verification workflow is stated. The page does not publish a code, separate waiver form, response time, application-cycle deadline, financial-hardship route, or universal eligibility.

Tufts’ current M.S. in Biomedical Engineering page confirms that the department is part of the School of Engineering. The next step is to verify the Ph.D. page before applying the shared School of Engineering policy to both published degree paths.

Sources: https://asegrad.tufts.edu/applying/application-fee-waivers ; https://asegrad.tufts.edu/program/biomedical-engineering-masters

## Confirmed doctoral scope and product validation

The current Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering page identifies the doctoral program in the Department of Biomedical Engineering and uses the same Arts & Sciences | Engineering Graduate Admissions pathway, confirming its School of Engineering scope. On August 26, 2026, loaded public doctoral and master’s profiles rendered the qualified School of Engineering waiver panel with the listed categories, online-application selection and verification-letter boundary, and explicit NESCAC exclusion. No blanket, hardship, code, form, deadline, or approval-time claim appeared.

Source: https://asegrad.tufts.edu/program/biomedical-engineering-doctorate
