Separately, the current Graduate School Upcoming Information Sessions page says attendees of online information sessions may be eligible to receive an application fee waiver and describes year-round sessions, including application guidance and funding information. The historical 2025 Graduate Programs Virtual Open House page explicitly listed Biomedical & Chemical Engineering (Ph.D.) in the Nov. 12 Health & STEM Sciences Virtual Open House and said attendees may be eligible for an application fee waiver, but that event page is historical and is not used as proof of a current event date.

The current Biomedical & Chemical Engineering page confirms the published profile is a Ph.D. program and lists EngineeringCAS, no GRE, and Fall 2027 deadlines. No general automatic waiver code, form, or BME-specific waiver policy is published on the current program page.
