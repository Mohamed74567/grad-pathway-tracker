# University of Utah Biomedical Engineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

The current official University of Utah Biomedical Engineering graduate-admissions page publishes degree-specific fees: International MS US$65, Domestic MS US$55, International PhD US$65, and Domestic PhD US$0. For the domestic Ph.D. route, it instructs applicants to enter the Graduate Referral Code `CE2021BIMEPHD` in the application’s Additional Information section. The page does not publish a waiver code or waiver route for either master’s fee; the M.S. records must retain their US$55 domestic and US$65 international fee treatment without borrowing the Ph.D. referral code.

The same page sets the Ph.D. priority deadline at December 1, with consideration through April 1, and M.S. consideration through April 1; after April 1, applications are subject to an additional US$30 late fee. The code route is directly published by BME and is doctoral-only by the fee table and placement. No central hardship form was applied because the BME page provides the explicit domestic Ph.D. zero-fee/code route and does not state that the referral code applies to M.S. applicants.

Source: https://www.bme.utah.edu/prospective-graduate-students/
