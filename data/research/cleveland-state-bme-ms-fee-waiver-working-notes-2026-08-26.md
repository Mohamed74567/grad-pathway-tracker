# Cleveland State Biomedical Engineering M.S. fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

Cleveland State’s official Biomedical Engineering M.S. page confirms the published degree is in the Chemical and Biomedical Engineering department. The official graduate application page says CSU is waiving the US$40 application fee normally required for degree, licensure, and certificate programs, but the wording is framed as a COVID response and does not provide a current graduate cycle or an M.S.-specific route. A separate current CSU application portal page states that CSU is waiving application fees through Fall 2027, but the visible waiver statements are attached to undergraduate and international application tiles rather than a clear graduate-specific policy.

Because the official graduate page is temporally ambiguous and the newer portal wording does not clearly establish a Biomedical Engineering M.S. scope, no fee-waiver code, form, or session guidance is added. Existing M.S. fee treatment remains unchanged pending an unambiguous current graduate policy.
