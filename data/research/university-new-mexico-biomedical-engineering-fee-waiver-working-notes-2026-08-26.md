
## Current BME domestic fee evidence

UNM Biomedical Engineering’s current domestic graduate-admissions page states that applicants must pay a US$60 application fee by credit card when submitting the application and that no application is processed until the correct fee is paid. It publishes the same BME Graduate Programs process and deadlines for the active published M.S. and Ph.D. paths, but no BME-specific fee-waiver code, form, session, eligible category, request workflow, or approval timeline. Separate UNM international admissions material says a few academic departments may offer waivers, but it does not identify Biomedical Engineering or supply a current BME route. The existing published BME profiles should retain US$60 fee treatment and no waiver panel.

Sources: https://bme.unm.edu/prospective-students/apply/graduate-admissions-domestic-students.html ; https://bme.unm.edu/prospective-students/apply/graduate-admissions-international-students.html ; https://international.unm.edu/fee-waivers-and-coupons/fee-waivers-and-coupons.html
