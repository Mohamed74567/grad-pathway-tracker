# UMass Lowell Biomedical Engineering Graduate Family — Working Official-Source Notes

**Review date:** 2026-08-21  
**Status:** Direct Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering and the distinct joint M.S. in Biomedical Engineering and Biotechnology are published after three-pass reconciliation. The joint BMEBT doctorate remains an unreviewed candidate and is not inferred from the M.S. record.

## Degree identity and scope

The current UMass Lowell Department of Biomedical Engineering page establishes a research-focused **Ph.D. in Engineering, Biomedical Engineering**, including Cellular and Tissue Engineering, Medical Device Design, and Biomechanics tracks. The department also participates in the UMass-system joint Biomedical Engineering and Biotechnology (BMEBT) master’s and doctoral degrees. The current BMEBT admissions requirements identify both master’s and doctoral applicants as biomedical/biotechnology applicants and administer applications through EngineeringCAS. Both degree pathways fall within the agreed biomedical-engineering scope.

## Admissions, fees, and tests

The current BMEBT official admissions page sets a US$75 EngineeringCAS application fee, two recommendations for master’s applicants and three for doctoral applicants, department-specific GRE policy, and TOEFL/IELTS policy for international applicants. The university Graduate Admissions catalog names Duolingo as an institutionally approved test but says thresholds are set by departments; the reviewed BMEBT page only explicitly requires TOEFL or IELTS. The profiles should therefore preserve a source-safe blank DET threshold unless a current program-specific acceptance rule is located.

The rendered direct BME Ph.D. page independently publishes its EngineeringCAS application links, US$75 fee, GRE-required doctoral policy, and TOEFL 79+/IELTS 6.5+/Duolingo 115+/ASC Level 6+/PTE 53+ English policy. It also states that the department strives to provide financial support for all admitted doctoral students through TA, RA, and summer research support, but makes all support contingent on availability. The published profile therefore records **available**, rather than guaranteed, funding; it leaves tuition and dated deadlines blank because the reviewed direct page does not give source-safe values.

The direct BME Ph.D. page’s own degree, research, admissions, and funding content is the primary source. Its 2027 EngineeringCAS route and fee/test facts corroborate the direct program treatment. The official BMEBT homepage is used solely for the visibly credited "Two students in Konow Lab" image, whose official page identification and laboratory context were visually verified.

## Joint BMEBT M.S. publication decision

The current BMEBT program page confirms the joint 31-credit M.S., Biomedical Sciences and Engineering options, 10-credit core including a laboratory, 21 credits of track-based electives with a three-credit research project, and full- or part-time study. The BMEBT financial-aid page states that a limited number of GTA/GRA positions may support graduate students. The resulting profile uses **available** funding rather than a guarantee and leaves dated deadlines and a Duolingo threshold blank.

The current BMEBT application page states a US$75 EngineeringCAS fee, two recommendations for master’s applicants, a qualified-candidate GRE-waiver possibility, and TOEFL/IELTS requirements for international applicants. The catalog retains a GRE 294 expectation and TOEFL iBT 79 minimum. Rather than choose between conflicting current official GRE treatments or manufacture a DET policy, the profile exposes the conflict, states both English-source boundaries, and leaves Duolingo blank.
