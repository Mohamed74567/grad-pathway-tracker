# University of North Texas M.S. in Biomedical Engineering — Official-Source Reconciliation

**Review date:** 2026-08-21  
**Decision:** Publish a distinct in-scope master’s profile with a thesis/non-thesis degree-path note and source-safe admissions boundaries.

## Pass 1 — Degree identity and scope

The current University of North Texas degree page establishes a master’s degree in Biomedical Engineering with thesis and non-thesis options. It names medical-device innovation, FDA-requirement facilitation, statistical analysis, independent research, and healthcare applications as program features. The degree is therefore in scope.

## Pass 2 — Admissions, tests, fee, and deadlines

UNT’s current Biomedical Engineering graduate-admissions page publishes domestic and international priority dates: Fall August 15 and Spring December 1 for both groups, plus Summer June 15 for domestic applicants. It states a BME-specific GRE waiver for qualified domestic ABET/similarly accredited science-program graduates with GPA at least 3.0 and qualified international engineering/science/biotechnology/computer-science graduates with GPA at least 3.7; remaining applicants need GRE scores. The current central Graduate CAS application page confirms a US$75 non-refundable fee.

Current UNT international admissions policy accepts DET 100, TOEFL iBT 79 before January 21, 2026 or 4 on the revised scale after that date, IELTS 6.0, PTE 53, and other listed options. It says scores normally expire after two years. The BME page lists TOEFL/IELTS but does not state a contrary DET rule; the profile will describe the current central policy with a program-may-have-additional-requirements boundary.

## Pass 3 — Funding, waiver, and image

The official BME funding page says teaching and research assistantships support many graduate students but limits eligibility to doctoral students and master’s students choosing the thesis option. The master’s profile therefore marks funding as available only for the thesis path, not guaranteed for all M.S. applicants. Current UNT support content identifies a McNair Scholar graduate-application waiver but no broader general waiver route was confirmed; the fee-waiver card will be limited accordingly. The profile uses a managed copy of the BME Research page’s laboratory image, visibly credited to **University of North Texas Department of Biomedical Engineering**.
