# San Diego State University Bioengineering Graduate Family — Official Source Reconciliation

**Review date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** Published after three-pass official-source reconciliation.

## Scope and degree structure

San Diego State University awards a distinct **M.S. in Bioengineering** through Mechanical Engineering. The current degree page describes a 30-unit, thesis-required master’s with biomechanics, biomaterials, and bioinstrumentation study areas, explicitly positioning the degree for medical-device and biotechnology careers. SDSU also awards, jointly with UC San Diego, a separate **Ph.D. in Engineering Science (Bioengineering)**. The doctoral candidate must apply through both institutions and receives a single jointly awarded doctorate. Both titles fall within the approved Bioengineering scope.

## Master’s admissions and source-safe treatment

The M.S. page explicitly requires the US$70 Cal State Apply fee, identifies Fall and Spring closing dates, and lists a supplemental application with résumé/CV, statement, and three recommendations. SDSU’s current central Fall 2027 graduate instructions publish its $70 non-refundable fee and the current baseline English options, including DET 105; the program is permitted to impose a higher standard. The central campus rule suspends the GRE requirement, but permits individual programs to differ; the profile preserves that qualification rather than saying the degree itself has permanently abolished the GRE.

The degree page does not publish an assistantship, scholarship, or guaranteed funding process. Its funding status is therefore **not stated**. SDSU’s central graduate instructions do publish a very limited financial-need fee-waiver appeal for California residents, so the M.S. alone carries a scoped, no-guarantee contact card.

## Joint doctorate admissions and funding

The current JDP page establishes Fall 2027 dates of October 1 to December 1, 2026; coordinated SDSU, SDSU-program, and UCSD applications; advisor confirmation; and a $70 non-refundable SDSU application fee. It further states that UCSD’s JDP fee is waived for all applicants, while the SDSU fee cannot be waived for international applicants. The profile presents the combined cost treatment rather than implying all fees are waived.

The JDP page says the GRE is not required but recommends specific benchmark scores, mandates TOEFL/IELTS/PTE when English evidence is required, and explicitly rejects Duolingo. It describes adviser-dependent financial support, limited competitive tuition waivers, and possible graduate/research assistantships—meaning support is **available**, not guaranteed.

## Image provenance

Both profiles use a managed-storage copy of an official SDSU College of Engineering Bioengineering Research Opportunities visual. The College separately confirms that the Engineering and Interdisciplinary Sciences Complex houses Bioengineering research laboratories. The visible credit is therefore **San Diego State University College of Engineering**.

## Live profile validation

Both detail routes resolved in the live application. The M.S. view displayed the US$70 fee, central DET 105 baseline, carefully qualified campus GRE policy, limited fee-waiver appeal, credited laboratory image, and doctoral degree switcher. The joint doctoral view separately displayed the December 1, 2026 deadline, SDSU versus UCSD fee treatment, recommended-not-required GRE scores, explicit Duolingo exclusion, qualified funding status, credited image, and master’s switcher. The first automated captures showed the app’s intentional loading frame before resolution; direct rendered-page validation confirmed both populated views.
