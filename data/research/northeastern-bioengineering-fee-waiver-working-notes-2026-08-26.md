# Northeastern Bioengineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

Northeastern College of Engineering’s current official graduate-admissions page states that Ph.D. applicants pay a US$100 application fee. It separately states that the fee is waived for all new applicants applying to any Master of Science or Graduate Certificate for Fall 2026 and Spring 2027. The published Bioengineering Ph.D. page repeats the US$100 fee requirement and links applicants to College of Engineering admissions. The page provides a narrow Iran-specific payment-delay contact for Ph.D. applicants, not a fee waiver.

The M.S. waiver is an automatic term-specific no-fee policy, not a code, form, or request route; it should not be represented as a future Fall 2027 waiver without a current source. The Ph.D. profile retains its paid-fee treatment and receives no unsupported waiver entry. The current directory cohort contains the Northeastern Bioengineering Ph.D. profile; no new M.S. waiver guidance is added.
