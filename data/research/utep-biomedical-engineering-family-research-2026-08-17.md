**Review date:** 2026-08-17

## Confirmed degree identity and scope

UTEP’s current Biomedical Engineering pages identify separate **M.S. in Biomedical Engineering** and **Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering** awards. The M.S. page describes advanced device design, regulation, entrepreneurship, and preparation for biomedical-device industry or doctoral study. The doctoral page describes interdisciplinary engineering applied to biomedical problems, including health disparities, microfluidics, musculoskeletal biomechanics, mobile health, cancer imaging diagnostics, tissue engineering, 3D-printed biomaterials, and neurosciences.

## Current admissions evidence captured

UTEP’s current deadline database lists **Fall 2027** final dates of **June 30, 2027** for Biomedical Engineering M.S. and **July 1, 2027** for Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. The catalog’s graduate admission policy publishes processing fees of **US$45** for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and Mexican nationals, and **US$80** for international applicants. It says programs may impose different requirements and may require a higher English score.

The central catalog accepts TOEFL, IELTS, PTE, and the Duolingo English Test as possible English-proficiency evidence, but directs applicants to the current testing page for required scores. The current testing page gives graduate baselines of TOEFL iBT 79 before January 21, 2026 or 4.5 on the revised scale after that date, IELTS 6.5, and Duolingo 110. The current College of Engineering graduate admissions table establishes that **GRE scores are optional** for both the Biomedical Engineering M.S. and Ph.D.; it also gives the current Fall domestic/Mexican-national deadline of July 1 and international deadline of February 1, plus their résumé, statement, and three-letter requirements. The degree-specific M.S. and Ph.D. pages say GRE scores will be considered and publish a 3.0 GPA expectation, but do not contradict the current optional designation.
