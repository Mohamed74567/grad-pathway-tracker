# Virginia Commonwealth University Biomedical Engineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

The current official VCU College of Engineering graduate FAQ states that applicants should use the Application Fee Waiver Request form and that email requests to the Office of Graduate Studies are not considered. The form URL is https://forms.gle/D3HkHrNaVvgH6cVM6. The College cannot grant waivers for all requests; approved waivers appear in the online application portal within five business days, and if no waiver appears after that time the request was denied. Duplicate requests are not accepted.

The current official VCU Engineering graduate admissions page publishes Biomedical Engineering as both an M.S. and Ph.D. offering and directs applicants to the same College-level admissions process. The reviewed official pages do not publish a financial-need category, guaranteed approval, degree-specific exclusion, code, or fixed request deadline. The route can be recorded separately on both published BME profiles, preserving approval uncertainty, five-business-day timing, no-duplicate-request, and central College scope.
