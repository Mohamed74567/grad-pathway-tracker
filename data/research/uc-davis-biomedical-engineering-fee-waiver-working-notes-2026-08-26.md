# UC Davis Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Confirmed Biomedical Engineering Graduate Group M.S./Ph.D. evidence

The current UC Davis Biomedical Engineering Graduate Group application-information page, last updated June 25, 2026, provides a shared Fall 2027 application workflow for doctoral and master’s studies. It lists the standard nonrefundable application fee as US$135 for U.S. residents and US$155 for all other applicants. It states that UC Davis fee waivers are available only to applicants who participated in specific eligible Graduate Preparation Programs.

To receive the waiver, an affiliated applicant must indicate participation and provide the name and contact information of the preparation-program coordinator in the relevant section of the online application. The page explicitly states that additional hardship fee waivers are not available. It gives the Fall 2027 application deadline as December 1, 2026 and requires all materials by December 15 for review, but does not publish a waiver-specific lead time, code, separate form, approval timeline, or international eligibility beyond the named eligible-program mechanism.

This supports shared M.S./Ph.D. guidance for the two published BME Graduate Group profiles only. The Medical Device Development M.Eng. is separately administered and requires a separate source review.

Source: https://bmegg.ucdavis.edu/application-information

## Confirmed Medical Device Development M.Eng. evidence

The Medical Device Development M.Eng. is separately administered. Its current FAQ, last updated April 3, 2026, states that an applicant may qualify to request an application-fee waiver by participating in UC Davis Graduate Preparation Programs and links to the official eligible-program list. Unlike the BME Graduate Group application page, this FAQ does not publish a completed-application workflow, coordinator-contact requirement, hardship boundary, fee amount, waiver-specific deadline, code, form, or approval timing. Guidance for this M.Eng. must therefore preserve only this narrower eligible-program statement and not inherit the BME Graduate Group mechanics.

Source: https://bme.ucdavis.edu/meng-medical-device-development-faq

## Product validation

On August 26, 2026, the loaded public Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. profile displayed the Graduate Preparation Program fee-waiver help panel with the nonrefundable US$135/US$155 fees, eligible-program-only scope, coordinator-information workflow, hardship exclusion, and December 1, 2026 application deadline. The visible panel was sourced only to the BME Graduate Group page.

The loaded Medical Device Development M.Eng. profile displayed only its own narrower eligible-program panel. It visibly excluded the BME Graduate Group’s coordinator-information, hardship, timing, and fee-mechanics claims.
