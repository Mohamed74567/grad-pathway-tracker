# University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee Biomedical Engineering fee-waiver working notes

## Official sources reviewed
- Graduate School admission: https://uwm.edu/graduateschool/students/admission/
- Graduate School Open House: https://uwm.edu/graduateschool/openhouse/
- Graduate School Preparation Pipeline Fee Grant: https://uwm.edu/graduateschool/students/financial-support/preparation-pipeline-fee-grant/
- College of Engineering & Applied Science graduate students: https://uwm.edu/engineering/students/future/graduate-students/

## Verified findings
UWM’s Graduate School admission page states that the standard graduate application fee is non-refundable US$75. It publishes three relevant routes: some program fee waivers contingent on registration and attendance at the in-person Graduate School Open House; limited Preparation Pipeline Fee Grants for participants in named graduate-preparation programs; and an automatic waiver for one doctoral-program application when the applicant already holds or will complete a UWM master’s degree before beginning the doctorate. The page also requires supporting documentation for pipeline grants at least 7–10 days before the program deadline.

The current Open House page schedules an in-person event for November 5, 2026, 5:00–7:00 p.m. at the Wisconsin Room in the UWM Student Union. The Graduate School will pay the application fee for prospective students who start an application between August 15, 2026 and March 31, 2027, physically attend the Open House, and follow the linked fee-waiver-document instructions. The page says this is an in-person event, not a virtual attendance route.

The current Preparation Pipeline Fee Grant page lists the eligible programs: AHA HBCU Scholar Recognition, Amgen Scholars, Big Ten SROP, Cientifico Latino, Gates Millennium, MCH-LEAP, Howard Karsh STEM Scholars, MMUF, NSF LSAMP, NSF REU, PPIA Junior Summer Institute, The Leadership Alliance, and UW–Madison SROP. The applicant must select the qualifying program in the UWM Graduate School application and upload a signed PDF on official letterhead containing the applicant’s name and program contact information. To ensure consideration, the application plus request and documents should be submitted at least ten business days before the program deadline.

The College of Engineering page describes both M.S. and Ph.D. graduate structures but does not publish a BME-specific fee code or separate waiver policy. UWM’s central routes are therefore applicable to the published BME degree paths only as central Graduate School routes, with the doctoral UWM-master’s exception retained Ph.D.-only. Assistantships and tuition waivers are funding, not application-fee waivers, and must not be represented as such.

## Implementation boundary
Use degree-separated guidance for the published UWM Biomedical Engineering M.S. and Ph.D. profiles. A shared open-house/pipeline route can be used on both where central policy supports graduate applicants, while the UWM-master’s automatic waiver must remain Ph.D.-only. Preserve the US$75 non-refundable fee, physical in-person Open House requirement, date window, named pipeline categories, documentation requirements, ten-business-day timing, and no invented code or automatic waiver claim beyond the specific UWM-master’s doctoral rule.
