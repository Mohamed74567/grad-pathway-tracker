# Vanderbilt Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Confirmed scope and eligibility

Vanderbilt Graduate School’s current application-requirements table identifies both Biomedical Engineering M.S. and Ph.D. programs as School of Engineering offerings. Its `Exempt` label refers to the GRE-requirement status, not an application-fee waiver, so it cannot be used as fee evidence.

The current Vanderbilt Engineering Graduate Programs page states that fee waivers are available to students currently enrolled in or who have graduated from U.S. institutions, as well as students from select international institutions. The page describes graduate engineering M.Eng., M.S., and Ph.D. programs and separately gives Ph.D. application timing, but the rendered waiver statement does not identify a waiver code, form, contact, automatic workflow, approval timing, full list of qualifying international institutions, or a degree-specific cycle end date.

The next review must identify whether Vanderbilt publishes usable application or contact mechanics. Any future guidance may cover the BME M.S. and Ph.D. only because both are explicitly listed as Engineering programs; it must retain the stated U.S.-institution/selected-international-institution eligibility and avoid claiming universal eligibility.

Sources: https://gradschool.vanderbilt.edu/admissions/apply/application-requirements-by-program/ ; https://engineering.vanderbilt.edu/graduate-admissions/

## Current Fall 2027 result

The current School of Engineering graduate page supplies the needed degree-specific detail. For Fall 2027, its published table sets **December 1, 2026** as the fee-waiver deadline for Ph.D. applicants and says fee waivers are unavailable after that date. It limits eligibility to students currently enrolled in or graduated from U.S. institutions plus students from select international institutions. It names no code, form, full selected-institution list, approval timeline, or request steps; the page lists `gradengineering@vanderbilt.edu` as the graduate contact.

The same table states **“No application fee for M.S. program”** with a March 15, 2027 final M.S. deadline. Accordingly, the product has a qualified Fall 2027 Ph.D.-only contact panel and leaves the M.S. without a waiver panel because its fee is already published as absent. The loaded public M.S. profile was validated to show “No application fee for the M.S.” and no doctoral waiver panel.
