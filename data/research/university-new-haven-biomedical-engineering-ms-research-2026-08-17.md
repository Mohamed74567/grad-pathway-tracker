# University of New Haven M.S. in Biomedical Engineering — Official-Source Reconciliation

**Review date:** 2026-08-17  
**Publication decision:** A distinct in-scope M.S. in Biomedical Engineering is supported by current University of New Haven program, graduate-admission, international-admission, deadline, assistantship, and official-image sources.

## Degree identity and curriculum

The University of New Haven’s current Tagliatela College of Engineering page describes a hands-on, career-focused **M.S. in Biomedical Engineering**. Its published examples include biostatistics, biomedical polymers, design of experiments, and mechanical engineering analysis; the page explicitly identifies the degree as master’s-level rather than a professional-engineering licensure pathway.

## Admissions and funding

The current Graduate Admissions checklist applies a US$50 non-refundable master’s application fee and specifies a waiver for current University of New Haven students and alumni. It lists two recommendations, statement of purpose, CV/résumé, transcripts, and a central policy under which only Forensic Science M.S. requires GRE/GMAT; the BME M.S. is therefore no-GRE under the reviewed current source.

For Engineering master’s students, the international policy sets TOEFL 75, IELTS 6.0, PTE 50, or DET 105. Current master’s priority guidance is domestic Fall March 1/April 1 and Spring October 1/November 1; international Fall May 1 and Spring October 15. The central assistantship/scholarship page makes full-time domestic and international master’s students eligible for competitive Provost’s Assistantships (75% tuition plus hourly wage) and Dean’s Scholarships (up to 50% tuition), each subject to published priority deadlines and selection.
