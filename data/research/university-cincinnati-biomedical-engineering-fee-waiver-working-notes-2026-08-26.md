# University of Cincinnati Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Current official boundary review

The College of Engineering and Applied Science (CEAS) Graduate Frequently Asked Questions page describes itself as guidance for applicants to graduate degree programs in the College of Engineering and Applied Science and states: **“CEAS does not offer application fee waivers.”** This is the controlling program-college source for the published Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. and M.S. profiles.

The current central Graduate Admissions FAQ exposes an admission-fee-waiver question but does not render the answer text in the initial content. Search discovery suggests a limited active-duty military/veteran category, but that must not be treated as a Biomedical Engineering or CEAS-applicable route without direct, current central wording and reconciliation against CEAS’s explicit exclusion. No product guidance has been added.

Sources: https://www.ceas.uc.edu/admissions/graduate/faqs.html ; https://www.admissions.uc.edu/apply/graduate/gradfaq.html

## Reconciled central-policy result

After expanding the current central Graduate Admissions FAQ, the listed waiver is limited to U.S. active-duty military members and veterans and is described as automatically applied on submission for qualifying applicants. However, CEAS’s graduate FAQ explicitly states that CEAS does not offer application-fee waivers. The central page does not identify Biomedical Engineering or override the CEAS-specific exclusion. Because the degree-family source conflicts with the broader central wording and no BME-specific exception is published, no BME M.S. or Ph.D. fee-waiver panel should be added.
