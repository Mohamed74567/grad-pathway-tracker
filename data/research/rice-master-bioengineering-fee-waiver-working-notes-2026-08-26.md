
## Confirmed degree boundary

Rice Bioengineering’s current Academics page states: “The graduate application fee has been waived for PhD applicants.” Its current graduate FAQ repeats: “The application fee has been waived for all PhD program applicants.” The same official department site separately identifies the Master of Bioengineering (MBE) program and publishes no MBE waiver, code, form, event, eligibility category, request workflow, timing, or approval route. The published Rice MBE profile therefore remains without fee-waiver guidance; the doctoral-only statement must not be transferred to the MBE.

Sources: https://bioengineering.rice.edu/academics ; https://bioengineering.rice.edu/academics/frequently-asked-questions ; https://bioengineering.rice.edu/academics/masters-programs
