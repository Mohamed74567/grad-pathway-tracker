## Verified findings

Caltech’s current Graduate Studies Office application page lists a US$100 application fee. It says fee waivers are granted to a limited number of applicants who demonstrate financial need or are affiliated with organizations that have made special arrangements with the office. The applicant FAQ states that the fee-waiver request form is included in the online application under payment options. Applicants requesting consideration for financial hardship provide earned income, expenses, and circumstances affecting their ability to pay. Requests are individually reviewed after the application is submitted, decisions are sent through the online admissions system, and applicants may submit additional documentation or payment if the waiver is not granted.

The Medical Engineering graduate page identifies a Ph.D.-leading graduate program, a December 15 application deadline, and expected annual support including tuition and stipend for admitted students. The page says students are not normally admitted toward a standalone M.S.; an M.S. may be awarded along the way toward a Ph.D. Therefore, the waiver route is applicable to the published Caltech Medical Engineering Ph.D. profile only. No separate M.S. policy is created or inferred.

## Safe application wording

Use a `fee_waiver_form` entry on the Caltech Medical Engineering Ph.D. profile. Describe the route as limited and non-guaranteed, with financial-need evidence or qualifying organizational affiliation, and direct applicants to the online application’s payment-options section. Do not invent a code, named eligibility category, approval timeline, or automatic approval. Preserve the US$100 fee and December 15 deadline separately as current official facts.

## Boundary

Do not transfer this route to a standalone M.S. profile. Caltech’s Medical Engineering page says students are not normally admitted toward the M.S.; the M.S. may be awarded en route to the Ph.D.
