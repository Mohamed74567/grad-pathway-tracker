# University of Dayton Bioengineering Discovery Conclusion — 2026-08-17

Current official University of Dayton sources do **not** establish a distinct active M.S. in Bioengineering suitable for publication. The apparent `/bioengineering-ms/` path resolves to the university-wide program finder rather than a degree-detail page. The current Graduate Catalog documents an **M.S. in Chemical Engineering** and a separate **Certificate in Bioengineering**; it describes Bioengineering as an area of study within Chemical Engineering, not as an independently awarded master’s degree. No Bioengineering M.S. profile was added.

Reviewed sources: [University program finder](https://udayton.edu/academics/programs/index.php), [Graduate Catalog: Chemical Engineering](https://catalog.udayton.edu/graduate/schoolofengineering/programsofstudy/chemicalengineering/), [Graduate application page](https://udayton.edu/apply/graduate/index.php).
