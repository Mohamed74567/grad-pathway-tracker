# UAB Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Current source reconciliation

UAB’s School of Engineering graduate material currently states that domestic applicants may receive a free application only when the application is complete by the applicable priority deadline, and refers applicants to central Graduate Admissions for further domestic fee-waiver information. It separately lists a US$60 fee for international applicants.

UAB Graduate Admissions defines domestic applicants as U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents, but the currently accessible domestic-applicant page does not expose a general fee-waiver category or request workflow in its static content. The Biomedical Engineering M.S. admissions page confirms that it uses the UAB Graduate School application portal and publishes a November 30 priority deadline and December 31 final deadline, but it does not state a fee waiver.

Before any product update, the doctoral BME page and a clear official School of Engineering or Graduate Admissions source must establish whether the complete-by-priority no-fee treatment applies to both BME degree paths and whether it carries a current application-cycle boundary. No broad central claim should be assigned until this is reconciled.

Sources: https://www.uab.edu/engineering/home/?view=category&id=76 ; https://www.uab.edu/gradadmissions/apply/domestic-applicants ; https://www.uab.edu/engineering/bme/graduate/msbme/msbme-admissions

## Confirmed shared BME deadline scope

The current UAB Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. admissions page uses the same Graduate School online application portal as the M.S. page and publishes the same November 30 priority deadline and December 31 final deadline. Both BME degree paths therefore sit within the School of Engineering’s stated complete-by-priority domestic-fee treatment. This needs a product update only if the School of Engineering wording is preserved as a **domestic, complete-by-priority** rule and does not imply that international applicants, incomplete applications, later applications, or unlisted domestic waiver categories are covered.

Source: https://www.uab.edu/engineering/bme/graduate/phd/phd-admissions

## Product validation

On August 26, 2026, loaded doctoral and master’s profiles displayed the same bounded application-fee treatment: domestic applicants are free only when complete by the November 30 priority deadline, otherwise US$50; international applicants are US$60. Neither profile displayed a speculative fee-waiver help panel or a claim about an unlisted domestic qualifying category.
