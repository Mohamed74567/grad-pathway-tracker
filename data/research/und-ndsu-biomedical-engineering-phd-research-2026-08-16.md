## Publication guardrails

This is an active joint doctoral program and a genuine legacy gap. Publish it under the established joint institution label **University of North Dakota / North Dakota State University** rather than as an NDSU-only degree. Preserve the catalog’s February 15 and September 15 dates as **annual guidance** without attaching an unverified future cycle. Use US$35, the published TOEFL/IELTS/Duolingo thresholds, qualified assistantship availability, the direct NDSU application, and a blank GRE field. The existing credited official NDSU Biomedical Engineering laboratory image may be reused as shared joint-program imagery with the same NDSU source provenance.
