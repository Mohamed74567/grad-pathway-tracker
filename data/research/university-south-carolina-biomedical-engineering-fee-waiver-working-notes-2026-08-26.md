# University of South Carolina Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Official fee-policy evidence

The current University of South Carolina Graduate School application page states that, effective August 1, 2026, the graduate application fee is US$50 for each primary application and that additional discipline-specific vendor fees may apply. It expressly states: “The Graduate School does not grant individual student requests for application fee waivers.”

The current Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. degree page identifies the major as Biomedical Engineering, the degree as Doctor of Philosophy, and the School/College as Engineering and Computing. It publishes Fall and Spring deadlines, required materials, and program contacts, but no Biomedical Engineering-specific application-fee waiver route. The published Biomedical Engineering M.S. and M.E. degree pages are governed by the same Graduate School application-fee statement in the current directory and do not publish a separate waiver exception.

The fee policy is current for applications submitted from August 1, 2026. No code, form, event, waiver-eligibility category, hardship process, approval timeline, or degree-specific exception is published for Biomedical Engineering Ph.D., M.S., or M.E. The three published paths should therefore retain their US$50 fee treatment and no fee-waiver panel.
