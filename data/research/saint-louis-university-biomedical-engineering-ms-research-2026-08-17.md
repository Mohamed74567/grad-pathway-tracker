# Saint Louis University Biomedical Engineering M.S. — Official Source Reconciliation

**Review date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** Published after three-pass official-source reconciliation.

## Pass 1 — Degree identity and scope

Saint Louis University’s current School of Science and Engineering page identifies a distinct **M.S. in Biomedical Engineering** in the Department of Biomedical Engineering. The current catalog independently confirms the degree and its thesis and non-thesis pathways. The record is in scope because its official title is Biomedical Engineering, not Biological Engineering.

## Pass 2 — Admission and cost treatment

The current catalog directs applicants to SLU’s direct graduate portal and identifies the degree-specific materials: transcripts, résumé/CV, professional-goal statement, and three recommendations for thesis applicants only. It also says international applicants must demonstrate English proficiency under SLU policy. The central SLU graduate policy publishes a direct-portal **no-application-fee** rule, while making clear that a separate centralized service could charge its own fee.

SLU’s central English policy publishes graduate baseline scores, including TOEFL iBT 80, IELTS 6.5, and Duolingo 110, while stating that graduate programs may require higher minimums. The profile therefore carries the central Duolingo minimum with the program-may-set-higher caveat. No current degree-specific page publishes a GRE rule or a dated admissions deadline, so both remain blank.

The catalog lists a **US$42,000 total program cost** and cautions that additional charges may apply. This is shown as tuition/cost information, not as an application fee.

## Pass 3 — Funding and image

The official catalog states that the School of Science and Engineering offers graduate fellowships and assistantships each year, and that assistantships provide tuition, stipend, and health insurance. Because it does not guarantee availability or a specific award to every M.S. applicant, the profile shows qualified funding availability rather than a funding promise.

The profile reuses the existing credited official SLU Biomedical Engineering laboratory image already used by the corresponding Ph.D. sibling, preserving its official source credit and avoiding a duplicate asset upload.
