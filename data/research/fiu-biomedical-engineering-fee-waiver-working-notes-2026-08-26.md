
## Central fee confirmation

FIU’s current graduate-admissions FAQ states under “Do I need to pay an application fee?” that students must pay a US$30 non-refundable fee for applications to be processed. The current Biomedical Engineering M.S. page identifies the CEC program and publishes no waiver route; the current FIU catalog identifies the doctoral program in the College of Engineering and Computing but publishes no fee-waiver route. The central FAQ likewise does not identify a Biomedical Engineering-specific waiver, form, code, session, eligibility category, or timing exception.

Sources: https://admissions.fiu.edu/how-to-apply/graduate-applicant/frequently-asked-questions/ ; https://cec.fiu.edu/degrees-programs/graduate/ms-in-biomedical-engineering/ ; https://catalog.fiu.edu/programs/BIOMEG:PHD
