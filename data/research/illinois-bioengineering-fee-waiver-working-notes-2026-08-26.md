# University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign Bioengineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

The current official Illinois Bioengineering Ph.D. admissions page publishes a limited number of departmental application-fee waivers for outstanding applicants who are U.S. citizens or permanent residents pursuing the Ph.D. in Bioengineering and who have experiences and skills that contribute to the department’s collegial and supportive academic and research environment. The page instructs qualifying applicants to answer “Yes” in the Application Fee Waiver section of the application and says applicants are informed in approximately 7–10 days whether the department approves the request or the applicant must pay.

The current official Illinois Bioengineering M.S. with thesis page separately publishes an application-fee-waiver section. The M.S. route is likewise described as limited departmental waivers for outstanding applicants who are U.S. citizens or permanent residents; request instructions and approval timing must remain attached to the M.S. record rather than inferred from the Ph.D. page. No code was published in the reviewed pages.

Sources: https://bioengineering.illinois.edu/admissions/graduate/phd ; https://bioengineering.illinois.edu/admissions/graduate/ms
