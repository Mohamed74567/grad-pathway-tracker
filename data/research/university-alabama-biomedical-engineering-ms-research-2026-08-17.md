# University of Alabama M.S. in Biomedical Engineering — Working Official-Source Notes

**Review date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** In-scope degree and core admissions evidence reconciled; publication pending final image provenance and record validation.

## Degree identity and structure

The University of Alabama catalog identifies a separately awarded **M.S. in Biomedical Engineering** within Chemical and Biological Engineering. It describes a 30-credit interdisciplinary curriculum emphasizing biomaterials, tissue engineering, drug delivery, polymers/plastics, nanotechnology, and bioprocessing. The catalog permits either a thesis Plan I or non-thesis Plan II path; the published BME curriculum includes core biomedical engineering coursework and a capstone experience.

## Admissions and policy evidence

The current UA Graduate School application-requirements page publishes a US$65 fee for U.S. citizens and permanent residents and US$80 for international applicants. It lists exemption categories for veterans/current U.S. military, previous UA Graduate School enrollees, qualifying UA undergraduates continuing in the same program, and UAH/UAB exchange students. It directs candidates to the program portal for degree-specific deadlines and testing.

The current Chemical and Biological Engineering graduate page directs applicants to UA Graduate School’s application and states that regular admission may be granted with a chemical-engineering degree, GRE General Test score of at least 300, and overall GPA above 3.0; applicants from related fields may be considered. Its current international admission treatment lists TOEFL 550 paper / 79 iBT, IELTS 6.5, or PTE 59. UA’s central current international policy accepts DET 120 with no subsection below 110, but says programs may impose a higher requirement; no BME-specific DET exception was located.

No reviewed BME degree or candidate-facing department page guarantees an assistantship, fellowship, or other support process for this specific master’s degree. Funding remains **not stated**. No active degree-specific deadline was found, so no planner date will be added.
