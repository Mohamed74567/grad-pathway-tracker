
## August 26, 2026 official-source review

The official ASU Degree Search page for Biomedical Engineering, MS lists “graduate admission application and application fee” among required materials and provides the program’s official Apply now route. It publishes no BME M.S.-specific waiver code, form, hardship category, admission-session route, deadline, or approval process. The ASU central graduate-admission page describes program-specific requirements and the application process but does not publish a BME-specific waiver route in the reviewed content. No transfer from a separate ASU department or from Ph.D. policy is permitted.

Sources reviewed: https://degrees.asu.edu/masters-phd/major/ASU00/ESBIOENMS/biomedical-engineering-ms ; https://admission.asu.edu/apply/graduate/admission. The BME department URL surfaced in search but did not resolve in this environment and was not used as evidence.
