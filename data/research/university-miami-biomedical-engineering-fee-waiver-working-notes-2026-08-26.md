# University of Miami Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Current official result

The University of Miami College of Engineering’s current Prospective PhD Students page requires a non-refundable US$85 application fee and publishes a Fall 2027 priority deadline of December 1; it provides no fee-waiver code, form, event, or eligibility route. The current Prospective Master's Students page likewise requires a non-refundable US$85 fee, provides a separate College of Engineering master’s-merit tuition-waiver statement that does not waive the application fee, and publishes its own Fall 2027 priority deadline of August 1; it provides no application-fee waiver route.

The master’s and doctoral pages are degree-specific College of Engineering sources. The product should retain the existing US$85 application-fee display on both published Biomedical Engineering profiles and should not convert tuition waivers into application-fee waivers or infer a central exception.

Sources: https://www.coe.miami.edu/academics/admissions/prospective-phd-students/index.html ; https://www.coe.miami.edu/academics/admissions/prospective-masters-students/index.html

## Product validation target

No database or UI change is expected unless a current official source publishes an application-fee waiver. The boundary review should be source-logged and checklist-tracked without adding guidance.
