# Virginia Tech Biomedical Engineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

The current official Virginia Tech Biomedical Engineering fee-waiver page says the Graduate School offers an application-fee waiver for qualifying applicants. Requests must be submitted through the Fee Waiver Request tab in the online application; approved codes are issued Monday–Friday during business hours, and applicants should allow 5–7 business days. Requests must be completed and approved before application submission; submitted fees are non-refundable. The department also says that if an applicant is not eligible under Graduate School criteria, a limited number of departmental waivers may be available by contacting Kelsey Wall or Mollie Plunkett and providing a CV/resume, transcripts, and TOEFL/IELTS if required.

The central Graduate School page lists documented qualifying categories, including military-related documentation, AmeriCorps, FAMU Graduate Feeder Scholars, Math Alliance, ESTEEMED, RISE, qualifying NSF/NIH-sponsored research experiences, and Virginia State University applicants. It states that financial-need waivers are not offered, one qualifying reason cannot be reused for another program/degree type, and departmental programs may supply their own code. The BME department page is shared for the Virginia Tech–Wake Forest BME graduate program and does not limit the route to one degree level. Any entry should therefore be added separately to each published BME degree profile, preserving approval uncertainty and no-refund/no-reuse limits.
