# University of Kentucky Biomedical Engineering — Duolingo Policy Lead

**Review date:** 2026-08-21  
**Status:** Completed and published. The Graduate School’s current rendered application-process page supplies the controlling graduate-specific evidence.

## Current search leads

Official University of Kentucky search results identified the [Pigman College of Engineering international-admission requirements](https://engr.uky.edu/admissions/international-admissions/admission-requirements) and central [UK International Center admission requirements](https://international.uky.edu/apply/admission-requirements) with surfaced Duolingo figures. Direct review establishes that the Engineering page routes to the **Undergraduate Admission Application**, while the International Center page expressly describes applicants completing secondary school and routes prospective graduate applicants elsewhere. Neither page supports a Biomedical Engineering M.S. or Ph.D. statement.

## Publication gate

The rendered [Graduate School application-process page](https://gradschool.uky.edu/admissions/application-process) states that all graduate applicants must show English proficiency and gives **Duolingo 115**, with possible ESL-professional evaluation. Neither reviewed BME source set a contrary standard, so this qualified central statement is published for both degree paths.
