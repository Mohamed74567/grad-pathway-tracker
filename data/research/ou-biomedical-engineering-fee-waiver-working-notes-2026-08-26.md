## Confirmed program-page mechanics

The saved official SBME page HTML contains the expanded **Application Fee Waiver Request** text. It instructs applicants who want to apply for an application-fee waiver to combine a CV, transcript(s), and statement of purpose into one PDF and email it to Graduate Program Coordinator Nicole Palmeter at `npalmeter@ou.edu`. It specifies November 15 as the deadline for a fall application-fee waiver and August 15 for a spring application-fee waiver.

The page positions this request in the shared Graduate Student Applications section after describing both the Biomedical Engineering M.S. and Ph.D. offerings. It does not state that a fee waiver is automatic, guaranteed, retroactive, or available after those dates. The current Graduate College Apply page identifies Biomedical Engineering in its admissions-counselor list and directs applicants to consult their academic unit, consistent with the department-level process. The next step is to preserve only these stated shared M.S./Ph.D. mechanics and limits; no broader central eligibility category should be inferred.
