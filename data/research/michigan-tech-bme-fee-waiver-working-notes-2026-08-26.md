# Michigan Tech Biomedical Engineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

Michigan Tech’s official Graduate School Biomedical Engineering MS, PhD page describes on-campus Biomedical Engineering master’s and doctoral programs and says applicants use a free graduate application. The Graduate School FAQ clarifies that there is no application fee, while international applicants must pay a $10 processing fee. The FAQ does not publish an application-fee waiver route; the processing fee is a separate charge and is not supported by a waiver claim in the reviewed sources.

The evidence applies to the BME degree family, but the published directory cohort contains only a Michigan Tech Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. profile for this focused queue. No fee-waiver guidance should be added because there is no application fee to waive and the international processing fee is distinct.

Sources: https://www.mtu.edu/gradschool/programs/degrees/biomedical-engineering/ ; https://www.mtu.edu/gradschool/prospective/apply-now/faq/
