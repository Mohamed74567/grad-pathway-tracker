# University of Louisville Bioengineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Current official evidence and directory boundary

The current UofL Graduate School fee-waiver page states that only military-connected students and students receiving U.S. government public assistance may be exempt from the graduate application fee with appropriate documentation. It also links an “Already Applied? Upload Your Fee Waiver Documentation Today!” status-portal route. The page separately says program-based fee waivers are occasionally granted by academic departments and directs applicants to their departmental admissions coordinator.

The current Speed School graduate and doctoral admissions page identifies Bioengineering M.S. and Translational Bioengineering Ph.D. pathways, but does not publish a Bioengineering-specific fee-waiver code, form, event, eligibility category, timing, or coordinator instruction. The narrow database cohort query returned no already-published UofL Bioengineering profile, so this review must not create a new profile or transfer the central policy to another existing record. No product data change is warranted in the current published-profile scope.
