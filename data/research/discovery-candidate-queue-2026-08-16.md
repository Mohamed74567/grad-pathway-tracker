## Verification gate

A candidate may move from this queue to the published directory only after: (1) the institutional program page confirms an eligible biomedical engineering, bioengineering, neuroengineering, or directly related graduate degree; (2) the institution’s current admissions/application page confirms the direct application route and available fee, deadline, test, and English facts; and (3) a separate official source confirms funding treatment or explicitly leaves it not stated. Each published field must retain its source URL, verification passes, and a checked date. Missing facts remain blank or `not_stated`; discovery-index tuition, acceptance, ranking, or fee claims are never copied into the verified record without separate attribution.

## State-by-state workflow

The queue is ordered to support a state-by-state pass: reconcile all eligible candidates in one state, record non-qualifying or duplicate outcomes, run link and source checks, then move to the next state. The next proposed pass is Arizona (University of Arizona), followed by Florida (FAMU–FSU, Miami, and South Florida), then Hawaii and Illinois. No candidate in this file is represented as verified or application-ready.

## State pass started: Arizona

The University of Arizona Biomedical Engineering M.S. and Ph.D. records were independently reconciled from the official BME Graduate Admissions, BME Graduate Degrees, and Graduate College international-admissions pages. The current BME page states Ph.D. Fall deadlines of January 6 for domestic applicants and December 1 for international applicants; the M.S. uses rolling review with priority dates of March 1 domestic Fall, September 1 domestic Spring, February 1 international Fall, and August 1 international Spring. The Graduate College page states TOEFL 79 before January 21, 2026 or 4.0 on the revised scale, IELTS 7 with no section below 6, and PTE 60; Duolingo is not accepted. TOEFL Essentials 9.5 applies to master’s programs, not doctoral programs. Existing source-backed fee, GRE, and funding treatments were retained, with Ph.D. funded and M.S. funding available.

Database records 1 and 2 were refreshed with these facts, annual deadline guidance, and three-pass provenance. No new candidate was published from the discovery queue; this is a refresh of existing Arizona coverage and the state pass remains open for additional Arizona institutions.

## Florida state pass: FAMU-FSU candidate review

Official sources reviewed: [FAMU-FSU CBE Graduate Admissions](https://eng.famu.fsu.edu/cbe/graduate-admissions), [FAMU-FSU Graduate Admissions Procedures](https://eng.famu.fsu.edu/cbe/graduate/graduate-admissions), [FAMU Graduate School prospective students](https://graduateschool.famu.edu/students/prospective.php), and [FAMU-FSU Engineering Graduate Programs](https://graduateschool.famu.edu/graduate-programs/graduate-programs-engineering.php). The CBE admissions page confirms Biomedical Engineering M.S. non-thesis and thesis routes, and lists final deadlines of November 1 for Spring/non-thesis, March 1 for Summer/non-thesis, and July 1 for Fall non-thesis and Fall thesis. It states the GRE requirement is waived for engineering master’s applicants through Fall 2026; the Ph.D. route allows a GRE waiver request rather than establishing a universal no-GRE policy. International English minimums listed by the joint CBE page are TOEFL 4.0 on the new scale or 550 paper, IELTS 6.5, PTE 55, Duolingo 125 for FSU or 120 for FAMU, Cambridge C1 180, and Michigan 55. The FAMU central page lists a US$30 non-refundable application fee, but FSU and FAMU routes are separate application systems; fee and funding treatment require degree- and university-specific reconciliation before publication. No new record is published from this candidate yet.
