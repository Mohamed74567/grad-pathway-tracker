# Texas A&M Biomedical Engineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

The current official Texas A&M BME graduate admissions FAQ states that the department provides limited fee waivers as coupon codes. Individual faculty provide them to selected applicants whom they intend to recruit into their lab; applicants should contact their intended faculty supervisor and ask about availability. The current BME admissions page separately states that all applicants must pay the Texas A&M application fee, so the coupon route is selective rather than automatic. The page publishes BME deadlines, including a December 1 Fall funding-priority deadline, but does not tie coupon issuance to a fixed date.

The FAQ speaks about BME graduate programs generally and does not restrict the route to M.S. or Ph.D.; the BME admissions page covers both degree paths. Any entry should therefore be added separately to both published BME degree profiles, explicitly identifying faculty selection and non-guaranteed availability, without inventing a code or implying that contacting a faculty member ensures approval.
