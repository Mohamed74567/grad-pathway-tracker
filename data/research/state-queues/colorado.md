## Colorado School of Mines Quantitative Biosciences and Engineering evidence

Colorado School of Mines officially offers Quantitative Biosciences and Engineering (QBE) Ph.D., M.S. Thesis, and M.S. Non-Thesis degrees. All three QBE degree types require no GRE, three recommendations for thesis/doctoral and two for non-thesis, statement of purpose, résumé, transcripts, and English-proficiency evidence when required. The QBE program is led by the Chemical and Biological Engineering department. Funding is not explicitly stated on the QBE page; the Chemical and Biological Engineering department page notes $7M/year in research funds and strong mentorship, suggesting competitive assistantship availability.
