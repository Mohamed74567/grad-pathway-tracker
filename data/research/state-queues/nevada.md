## University of Nevada, Reno Biomedical Engineering evidence

UNR officially awards Biomedical Engineering M.S. and Ph.D. degrees. Its departmental page supplies both annual Fall February 1 and Spring November 1 guidance, 3.0 GPA, GRE 301, and international TOEFL 79/IELTS 6.5/Duolingo 115 requirements. It distinguishes Plan A thesis and Plan B coursework M.S. tracks; only the thesis option may receive graduate assistantships. Ph.D. students and thesis M.S. students are eligible for department assistantships, which include tuition, fees, and living stipend when awarded. The department offers a narrowly defined Ph.D. fee-waiver route for high-scoring applicants but does not otherwise state a general fee amount. This page provides degree, admissions, direct-application direction, degree-specific funding, and research evidence; official imagery remains needed.
