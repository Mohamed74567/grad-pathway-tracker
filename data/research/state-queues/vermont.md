# Vermont — State-by-State Biomedical Engineering Candidate Queue

## Scope

Enumerate Vermont graduate programs in biomedical engineering, bioengineering, neuroengineering, and directly adjacent engineering research disciplines. A profile is publishable only after three-pass reconciliation of official institutional sources. Unknown admissions, funding, fee, test, tuition, ranking, and acceptance facts remain blank rather than inferred.

## Required evidence per published profile

1. Official degree identity and overview.
2. Official admissions guidance and application route.
3. Official curriculum or research source.
4. Official funding treatment, when stated.
5. Credited official or clearly licensed university imagery.
