## West Virginia University Biomedical Engineering evidence

WVU’s official catalog and Chemical and Biomedical Engineering pages confirm separately awarded M.S.Bm.E. and Ph.D. degrees. The M.S. is research-based with thesis or problem report; the Ph.D. is research-intensive with qualifying, candidacy, final examinations, and dissertation defense. The catalog’s 2027–2028 admission section requires a 3.0 GPA, transcripts, CV, statement of purpose, three recommendations, and WVU English proficiency where applicable. The department’s current graduate page states GRE is not required, January 15 Fall and August 15 Spring priority timing, and full tuition waiver plus competitive stipend for all doctoral students; the M.S. does not receive a universal award. Credited WVU image acquisition plus fee/English/Duolingo/direct-application confirmation remain required before publication.
