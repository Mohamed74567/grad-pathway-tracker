## University of Kentucky Biomedical Engineering evidence

University of Kentucky’s official BME page confirms separately awarded M.S. and Ph.D. degrees. The M.S. offers a 30-credit thesis Option A and 31-credit non-thesis Option B; the Ph.D. requires original research and dissertation defense. The College of Engineering graduate admissions page directs applicants to the UK Graduate School, states US$65 domestic/US$75 international fees, requires a CV, personal statement, and three recommendations, and refers applicants to the official program source for timing and requirements. That page lists GRE and TOEFL/IELTS as required information at the College level; BME-specific exceptions are still unconfirmed. UK’s Department of Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. fellowship listing states a US$20,000 stipend for doctoral students selected by the program. Degree-specific annual dates, Duolingo treatment, and credited imagery remain required before records can be published.

## University of Louisville Bioengineering evidence

The University of Louisville’s Translational Bioengineering Ph.D. page confirms rolling August/January/May admissions, an official direct application, full financial support for up to four years subject to good standing, priority early-January funding consideration, and TOEFL 79/IELTS 6.5/Duolingo 105 treatment. The M.S. catalog has a direct Graduate School application, two recommendations, typical 3.0 GPA, and the same English scores/exemption context; it distinguishes thesis and non-thesis/project pathways. The inherited M.S. profile must receive a fifth verified source category and refreshed admission snapshot; the existing credited Louisville campus image remains an official source.
