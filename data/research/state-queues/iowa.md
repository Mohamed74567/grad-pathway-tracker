## University of Iowa Biomedical Engineering reconciliation evidence

The University of Iowa’s official Graduate Admissions page confirms separately awarded BME M.S. (thesis or non-thesis) and Ph.D. degrees, annual Fall January 1, Spring October 15, and Summer January 1 guidance, US$60 domestic/US$100 international fees, no-GRE treatment, direct online application, résumé, statement of purpose, and three recommendations. The department’s admissions overview specifies 3.0 M.S./3.25 Ph.D. GPA and TOEFL iBT 85 treatment. It distinguishes funding: most Ph.D. students receive faculty-supported GRAs, while most M.S. students do not; the non-thesis M.S. specifically carries no financial support. The existing Graduate Admissions campus image remains credited and source-backed. Iowa State’s official BME materials reviewed so far identify undergraduate rather than separately awarded BME graduate degrees; no unsupported Iowa State record will be published without further proof.
