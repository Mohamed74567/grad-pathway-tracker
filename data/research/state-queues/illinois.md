## Northwestern Biomedical Engineering refresh findings

Northwestern’s official BME doctoral page publishes fall-only admission, an annual December 1 deadline, online Graduate School application, three recommendations, academic/personal statements, résumé/CV, PI statement, optional writing samples, no accepted GRE scores, and English evidence of TOEFL, IELTS, MET, or a qualifying English-instruction degree. It states all admitted doctoral students are supported through fellowships, external fellowships, research assistantships, or teaching assistantships. The live M.S. page provides Winter 2027 November 1, Spring 2027 January 31, and Fall 2027 May 1 deadlines; the same no-GRE rule and English policy; course-only or thesis-path selection; and explicit self-funded/no-M.S.-assistantship treatment. The Graduate School’s current fee page lists US$95 with qualified domestic Ph.D./MFA fee-waiver treatment; its policy says the BME department does not issue fee waivers and international applicants are not eligible for TGS waivers. Sources: https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/biomedical/academics/graduate/phd/admissions.html, https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/biomedical/academics/graduate/masters/admissions.html, https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/academics/graduate/prospective-student-resources/financial-aid.html, and https://www.tgs.northwestern.edu/admission/application-procedures/application-requirements/fee.html.

## UIUC Bioengineering refresh findings

UIUC’s official Bioengineering doctoral page provides a five-year Ph.D. funding guarantee for fall/spring appointments that includes full tuition waiver, partial fee waiver, and stipend, subject to good standing and assistantship duties. The same page provides fall-only admission, annual August 1 opening, December 1 complete-application deadline, US$90 fee, optional GRE, three recommendation letters, materials checklist, and qualified U.S.-citizen/permanent-resident Ph.D. fee-waiver process. The thesis M.S. page identifies a typical two-year coursework, research, and thesis structure; the same annual fall-only December 1 timing and US$90 fee; optional GRE; and eligible-but-not-guaranteed RA/TA tuition-waiver appointments. UIUC Graduate College sources provide accepted full-status admission minima: TOEFL iBT 103 before January 21, 2026 (or 5.0 after), IELTS 7.5, and Duolingo 135, subject to exemption and potential program-specific thresholds. Sources: https://bioengineering.illinois.edu/admissions/graduate/phd, https://bioengineering.illinois.edu/admissions/graduate/ms, https://grad.illinois.edu/admissions/instructions/04c, and https://bioengineering.illinois.edu/admissions/graduate.

Both profiles are now refreshed to six source categories at three passes and were visually verified with their retained credited official UIUC campus hero.
