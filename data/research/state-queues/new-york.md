# New York State Queue

## Scope and operating rule

New York is the fourth state in the systematic directory workflow. The first research pass prioritizes legacy-listed programs that remain absent from the published directory. A profile can be published only after official program, admissions, and degree/curriculum sources reconcile its degree identity, application route, and source-safe facts. Each released profile must carry at least five source-field categories at three verification passes and a visibly credited real campus or facility image.
