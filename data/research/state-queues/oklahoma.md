## University of Oklahoma Biomedical Engineering evidence

OU’s Stephenson School of Biomedical Engineering officially offers a 30-credit thesis/non-thesis M.S. and a Ph.D. that can be entered directly from a B.S.; the Ph.D. includes post-baccalaureate coursework, general examination, and dissertation defense. Its program page publishes December 1 Fall and September 1 Spring guidance, 3.0 GPA, three recommendations, no-GRE treatment, and its own November 15/ August 15 fee-waiver deadlines. OU Graduate College lists US$50 U.S. citizen/permanent-resident and US$100 international application fees. English-test thresholds and degree-specific funding remain blank until separately confirmed. Gallogly Hall, home to the Stephenson School of Biomedical Engineering, is documented in the official College of Engineering facilities page; the prepared profile asset is `/manus-storage/ou-gallogly-hall_0d5c34d1.jpg`.
