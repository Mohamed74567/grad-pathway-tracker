## University of Maryland Bioengineering refresh findings

UMD’s official Fischell Department of Bioengineering admissions page identifies both Ph.D. and M.S. application routes, a science/engineering bachelor’s prerequisite, Calculus I and Differential Equations at enrollment, a required online application, transcripts, research-goals statement, minimum three recommendation providers, résumé/CV, and optional GRE. Its then-published Fall 2026 January 15 final and December 15 preferred dates are retained only as annual guidance, not active planner dates. The page sets a US$75 application fee with preferred-deadline financial-support/fee-waiver context and establishes TOEFL/IELTS-only international test treatment; no Duolingo policy is asserted. Official financial-support guidance commits to supporting Ph.D. students through their studies and describes a first-year fellowship (approximately US$40K, tuition remission, and subsidized health benefits) renewed through satisfactory progress and later advisor research assistantships. No equivalent M.S. funding statement appears in the reviewed evidence. The official research page provides a BIOE REU student image at https://bioe.umd.edu/sites/bioe.umd.edu/files/reu800x500.jpg. Sources: https://bioe.umd.edu/graduate/prospective-students/admissions, https://bioe.umd.edu/graduate/prospective-students/financial-support, https://bioe.umd.edu/graduate, and https://bioe.umd.edu/research.

Both profiles are now published to the six-source, three-pass standard and were visually verified with the credited official REU-student hero.

## Johns Hopkins Biomedical Engineering refresh findings

The official JHU BME doctoral admissions page identifies seven recruiting areas—Biomedical Data Science, Computational Medicine, Genomics & Systems Biology, Imaging & Medical Devices, Immunoengineering, Neuroengineering, and Translational Cell & Tissue Engineering—and states that all matriculating students receive a yearly stipend, full tuition, and medical/dental insurance. The official M.S.E. page offers course-based (one year) and thesis-based (one year coursework plus a second research year) paths; it confirms potential but never guaranteed PI support for second-year research, and BME TA availability only when announced. Its current page lists fall-only January 15, 2027 admission, three recommendations, a statement, résumé, and TOEFL/IELTS/Cambridge English for foreign students, with optional GRE that does not factor into review. Whiting Engineering central sources state no master’s application fee and US$75 doctoral fee plus qualified waiver process, while its published 2026 GRE general policy is not represented as an active-cycle BME claim. Sources: https://www.bme.jhu.edu/academics/graduate/phd-program/apply-to-the-phd-program/, https://www.bme.jhu.edu/academics/graduate/masters-programs/masters-program/apply-to-the-masters-program/, https://www.bme.jhu.edu/academics/graduate/masters-programs/masters-program/, and https://engineering.jhu.edu/admissions/graduate-admissions/full-time-programs/how-to-apply/general-application-requirements/.

Both profiles are now refreshed to the three-pass standard and visually verified with their retained credited official Johns Hopkins campus hero.
