## Montana State University findings — August 15, 2026

The M.S. in Bioengineering is a distinct 30-credit thesis degree with at least 10 thesis credits, a thesis defense, and an individually approved program of study. Official sources identify human-health, biomedical diagnostics, bioprocessing, biofilms, biotransport, microfluidics, disease diagnostics, energy, and environmental applications. The separate M.Eng. in Bioengineering is a 30-credit professional degree without a thesis or professional paper, typically completed within 18 months; it applies engineering methods to bioprocessing, human health, energy, and environment.

The Chemical and Biological Engineering department publishes 3.0 GPA; no-GRE; TOEFL 4.2 on the new scale / 80 iBT, IELTS 6.5, or Duolingo 120; assistantship-consideration dates of February 1 for Fall and August 1 for Spring; and domestic/international annual deadlines. It also distinguishes funding: the department and faculty strive to support thesis-option M.S. students through GRA/GTA opportunities, while M.Eng. programs are self-funded and generally not eligible for departmental funding. Central Graduate School application source confirms direct application and US$70 first-application fee. A relevant official Montana State research image remains to be staged before publication.
