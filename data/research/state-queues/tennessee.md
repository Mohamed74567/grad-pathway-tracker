## Vanderbilt Biomedical Engineering discovery

Vanderbilt’s official BME materials identify M.Eng., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees. Its official BME graduate-program page states that GRE scores are neither required nor used for evaluation of master’s or doctoral applications. Vanderbilt Graduate School’s program-specific requirements page lists Biomedical Engineering M.S. and Ph.D. with exempt testing treatment and annual March 15 M.S. / January 1 Ph.D. deadlines in the current source. A separate BME degree, funding, English/Duolingo, direct-application, and credited-image reconciliation pass remains required before refreshing the inherited Ph.D. or adding master’s records.
