## Purdue Biomedical Engineering evidence

Purdue’s official Weldon School admissions page identifies three distinct BME graduate routes: the Ph.D., thesis M.S., and coursework-only Professional M.S. The Ph.D. and thesis M.S. have December 1 Fall final and September 15 Spring guidance; the Professional M.S. has December 1 Fall priority with rolling review through April 15, September 15 Spring, and February 15 Summer guidance. Purdue directs both research degrees to five research areas: computational biomedicine; engineering biomaterials and biomechanics; imaging; instrumentation; and neuroengineering and neurotechnology. The professional-degree page confirms six coursework-only pathways and no-GRE treatment; it also supplies exact TOEFL/IELTS requirements. The program’s published pages require a current fee but do not state its amount, so a later central-admissions pass is needed for fee treatment.

## University of Notre Dame Bioengineering Ph.D. fee-waiver refresh — August 27, 2026

Notre Dame’s current Graduate School fee page states that each application has a US$75 fee and that Graduate School-administered degree programs may receive waivers. Published bases include documented financial need or hardship, active-duty U.S. military or veteran status, application to a listed national fellowship, and academic-department discretion. Financial-need and national-fellowship requests require documentation uploaded in the application and are generally processed within 48 hours; applicants should submit the application rather than wait for approval. The Bioengineering application page and College of Engineering page establish the reviewed pathway as a Ph.D. program with a December 31 deadline. No department-specific code is published, and no M.S. policy is inferred from this Ph.D.-only source set.
