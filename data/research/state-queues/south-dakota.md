## South Dakota Mines Biomedical Engineering M.S. findings — August 15, 2026

The official South Dakota Mines program page identifies a separately awarded Biomedical Engineering M.S. with thesis and non-thesis options plus an accelerated option. Thesis structure is 30 credits with 20 course and 10 research credits; non-thesis structure is 32 credits with 27–32 course credits and 0–6 project credits. The page identifies in-person delivery, laboratory research, medical devices, biomaterials, imaging, anatomy/physiology, and experimental design/data analysis.

South Dakota Mines application instructions distinguish thesis/research and non-thesis admissions. For Fall, thesis M.S./Ph.D. research applications have a February 1 deadline, non-thesis campus April 1, and distance August 1; Spring deadlines are August 1 for thesis/research, October 1 for non-thesis campus, and January 1 for distance. Thesis applicants submit a US$40 fee, statement of purpose/research interests, CV, three recommendations, official transcripts, GRE report, and English scores where applicable; successful applicants typically have GRE scores above the 50th percentile, but the GRE is waived for all current South Dakota Mines students and graduates. Non-thesis applicants submit a US$40 fee, CV, statement, unofficial transcripts, and English scores where applicable and are typically not considered for assistantship funding.

South Dakota Mines central English standards are Duolingo 105, IELTS 6.5, PTE 56, TOEFL iBT 79 (120 scale), or TOEFL 4.0 (6.0 scale). Thesis applicants are eligible for assistantship consideration based on the application and available funding; the official assistantship page states approximately 50% of graduate students receive assistantships, primarily thesis M.S. and Ph.D. research students, with tuition reduction covering 100% of tuition and program fees for campus courses. Non-thesis students are typically self-funded and not considered for TA/RA funding, though fellowships and federal financial aid may be available to eligible students.

The official Biomedical Engineering M.S. page supplies a credited image of a lab sample under a microscope for the profile hero. The record is ready for separate publication after duplicate checks and image upload.
