### Texas A&M Biomedical Engineering refresh findings

Texas A&M’s official BME degree pages identify a 64- or 96-hour dissertation-based Ph.D. depending on prior degree status and a 32-hour thesis M.S. with 24 formal-coursework hours. The department states that all admitted doctoral students receive multi-year funding offers, while master’s students may be considered for assistantships and all applicants are evaluated through EngineeringCAS. The current BME admissions page lists annual term/residency windows, optional GRE treatment, an application statement, unofficial transcripts, three letters, résumé/CV, EngineeringCAS, and funding-priority December 1 guidance. Its official funding page specifies that assistantship packages include a stipend starting at US$31,500, resident tuition/allowable-fee payment, and health benefits. Texas A&M International Graduate Admissions lists university-level TOEFL iBT 80 before January 21, 2026 or 4.5 afterward with 4.0 per skill, TOEFL Essentials 8.5, and IELTS 6.0. Its reviewed list does not include Duolingo, so no Duolingo policy is claimed. Both profiles are now published to the three-pass standard with retained credited Texas A&M imagery. Sources: https://engineering.tamu.edu/biomedical/academics/degrees/graduate/phd.html, https://engineering.tamu.edu/biomedical/academics/degrees/graduate/ms.html, https://engineering.tamu.edu/biomedical/admissions-and-aid/graduate-admissions/index.html, https://engineering.tamu.edu/biomedical/admissions-and-aid/scholarships-aid/graduate-funding/index.html, and https://admissions.tamu.edu/apply/international/international-graduate.html.

### UT Austin Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. refresh findings

UT Austin’s official BME doctoral page confirms a personalized learning plan, qualifying exam by the end of year one, proposal between years two and three, teaching requirement, and final oral defense typically in year five. The department publishes an annual December 1 doctoral deadline, fall-only admission, TOEFL 100 recommended where required, and no IELTS substitution. Central Graduate School information supplies US$65 domestic/US$90 international fees and qualified domestic fee-waiver treatment. BME funding is accurately represented as available because the department reports approximately 95% of doctoral students receive full support, contingent on funds and satisfactory progress. GRE and Duolingo remain deliberately blank because the reviewed current BME admissions policy does not make a program-specific claim. The retained credited official campus hero rendered correctly in public profile validation. Sources: https://bme.utexas.edu/academics/graduate-program/phd-program/, https://bme.utexas.edu/academics/graduate-program/admissions/, https://bme.utexas.edu/academics/graduate-program/funding/, and https://gradschool.utexas.edu/admissions/apply.
