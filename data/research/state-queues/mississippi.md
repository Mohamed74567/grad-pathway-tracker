## Mississippi State Biomedical Engineering evidence

Mississippi State’s official catalog confirms separately awarded Biomedical Engineering M.S. and Ph.D. degrees administered through Agricultural and Biological Engineering. The M.S. requires 24 coursework credits plus at least 6 thesis-research credits and an oral comprehensive examination; the Ph.D. includes preliminary examination, dissertation, oral defense, and advanced course/research hours. The catalog requires a 3.0 GPA, GRE scores, and TOEFL 96 iBT/IELTS 7.5 where applicable. Its departmental doctoral page identifies cardiovascular treatment, tissue-mechanics modeling, and cartilage/bone-regeneration research. A direct application, current fee, funding, and credited-image pass is still required before publication.
