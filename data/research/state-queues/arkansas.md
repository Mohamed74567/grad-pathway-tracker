# Arkansas — State-by-State Biomedical Engineering Candidate Queue

## Scope

Enumerate Arkansas graduate programs in biomedical engineering, bioengineering, neuroengineering, and directly adjacent engineering research disciplines. A profile is publishable only after three-pass reconciliation of official institutional sources. Unknown admissions, funding, fee, test, tuition, ranking, and acceptance facts remain blank rather than inferred.
