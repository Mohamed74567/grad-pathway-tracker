## Brown University Biomedical Engineering evidence

Brown officially offers a Biomedical Engineering Ph.D., as well as separate thesis-bearing Sc.M. and coursework-only M.Eng. master’s degrees. The doctoral admissions page establishes an annual December 1 deadline, optional GRE, three letters, Graduate School application, and a guaranteed five-year doctoral package including stipend, tuition remission, health-services fee, and health-insurance subsidy. The master’s page supplies annual admission guidance: Fall February 1 priority, April 15 international/final and fifth-year treatment, May 1 fifth-year, and Spring October 1 application treatment; it states neither GRE subject nor general is required. It also requires TOEFL/IELTS for covered international applicants but does not mention Duolingo. Master’s support is limited need- and merit-based tuition scholarships rather than a guarantee. Brown Engineering’s FAQ page supplies a university-controlled graduate laboratory photograph as a potential credited hero.

## University of Rhode Island eligibility determination

URI’s official Biomedical Engineering department states that its biomedical students have pathways into M.S. and Ph.D. programs in **Electrical Engineering** and lists B.S., minor, five-year bachelor’s-to-master’s, and graduate-certificate offerings. The reviewed official URI material does not identify a standalone BME, Bioengineering, or Neuroengineering M.S./Ph.D. degree. URI Electrical Engineering pathways therefore remain outside this cohort’s separately awarded Biomedical Engineering directory rule, and no unsupported URI profile is published.
