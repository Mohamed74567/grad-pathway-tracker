## Guardrails

Massachusetts includes joint and interdisciplinary degrees. The Harvard–MIT HST record must remain clearly labeled as a joint Medical Engineering and Medical Physics doctorate rather than an MIT-only BME degree. No historic deadline, fee, funding, acceptance-rate, tuition, or test-policy claim may be surfaced without a matching current official source.

## Research notes

Research sources: https://be.mit.edu/academic-programs/phd-program/ and https://oge.mit.edu/graduate-admissions/applications/.
