## Research notes

MIT Biological Engineering’s current official graduate page establishes a Ph.D.-only annual fall-admission process, two first-year core courses plus four electives, first-year fall/winter research rotations, faculty-advisor matching, a third-year fall thesis proposal, teaching experience, written thesis, and oral defense. MIT central Graduate Admissions directs applicants to department-specific requirements and the unified application portal. Research sources: https://be.mit.edu/academic-programs/phd-program/ and https://oge.mit.edu/graduate-admissions/applications/.

HST’s live official MEMP search results establish that the Medical Engineering and Medical Physics doctorate is a Harvard–MIT joint program. Official HST and MIT OGE listings identify September 1 opening, December 1 at 11:59 PM Eastern annual deadline guidance, US$90 fee, full funding for students in good academic standing, and MIT centralized application process. Research seeds: https://hst.mit.edu/applying-hst/applying-medical-engineering-and-medical-physics-memp-phd-program, https://hst.mit.edu/academic-programs/memp, https://hst.mit.edu/academic-programs/financial-support/MEMPs, and https://oge.mit.edu/programs/harvard-mit-health-sciences-and-technology/.
