## University of Arizona Biomedical Engineering evidence

The official Biomedical Engineering admissions page establishes degree-specific annual admission windows: Fall Ph.D. January 6 domestic/December 1 international, Fall M.S. March 1 domestic/February 1 international, and Spring M.S. September 1 domestic/August 1 international. It also establishes rolling M.S. review, transcripts, statement, three Ph.D. or two M.S. recommendations, calculus/differential-equation/life-science preparation, and TOEFL 79/IELTS 7.0/PTE 60/CESL or CEPT alternatives. The funding page guarantees stipend, in-state/out-of-state tuition, and health insurance for full-time doctoral students in good standing; M.S. assistantships are handled case by case and scholarships/fellowships may be available. The official UArizona degree-search page provides credited Biomedical Engineering M.S. imagery and an official program-level M.S. acceptance rate, which should only be retained with its visible source label and scope.

## Arizona State University Medical Engineering M.S. evidence

ASU’s official Medical Engineering M.S. is a directly related 30-credit interdisciplinary degree at the intersection of engineering and clinical medicine. It offers engineering-student and medical-student options, requires a capstone design/implementation sequence, and emphasizes digital health, medical devices, diagnostics, therapeutics, health-care artificial intelligence, clinical immersion, and translation from concept to clinic. The degree page establishes a June 1 final month-and-day deadline, US$75 domestic/qualified international fee treatment, direct ASU application, no required GRE claim, TOEFL 90/IELTS 7.0/Duolingo 115 entry requirements, and general ASU financial-aid direction without a departmental funding promise. The official SBHSE laboratory image may be reused with its visible school-level credit.
