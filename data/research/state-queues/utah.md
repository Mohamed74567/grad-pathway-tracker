## University of Utah Biomedical Engineering evidence

The official BME admissions page provides current annual guidance: Ph.D. priority deadline December 1, continuing space-available review through April 1; M.S. applications considered continually through April 1. It supplies the direct Slate application, source-backed GRE treatment (highly recommended but an application may be considered without it), international testing treatment (TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo), and BME-published minimums of TOEFL 90, IELTS 7.5, or Duolingo 125. The page distinguishes funding: most BME Ph.D. students are supported by faculty RAships; M.S. students are generally expected to self-fund, with some faculty potentially supporting thesis M.S. students. The BME support page documents research fellowships, RAships, TAships, extramural fellowships, tuition-waiver terms, and continuity conditions. A source-safe fee remains blank because the degree page’s fee section did not expose the amount in extracted content.

Its official track page establishes course-only, project, and thesis M.S. options; research specializations; and typical funding for Ph.D. students and thesis M.S. students through TAships, RAships, and fellowships. The official ECE Ph.D. catalog establishes holistic review, GRE not required (but potentially considered), international testing reference, and recommended TOEFL 90/IELTS 7.5/Duolingo 125 treatment. ## Utah State University Biological Engineering discovery

Utah State officially offers research-based Biological Engineering M.S. (Plan A thesis or Plan B research report) and Ph.D. degrees at Logan, with biomedical engineering, tissue/cellular engineering, biosensors, biophotonics, and bioMEMS among the documented research areas. Its current engineering program page calls for a bachelor’s degree, 3.0 GPA, TOEFL or IELTS, transcripts, three recommendations, and statement of purpose, and directs prospective students to contact the program about current funding and enrollment opportunities. A second official degree page shows rolling admission, US$55 fee, and an older GRE-score requirement; because the two official pages conflict or omit the current GRE rule, GRE will remain blank unless current source resolution is found. This candidate requires a contemporary direct-application, funding, test-policy, and credited-image pass before publication.
