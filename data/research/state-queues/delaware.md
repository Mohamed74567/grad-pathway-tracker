## University of Delaware Biomedical Engineering findings

University of Delaware’s official graduate pages identify the Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. as the department’s standalone BME graduate degree. It has a 39-credit doctoral curriculum, qualifying and candidacy milestones, a teaching-aid requirement, and research across biomechanics, drug delivery/nanomedicine, cell and tissue engineering, disease modeling, and neuroengineering/rehabilitation. The program page identifies annual December 15 admission timing for students intending to matriculate the following Fall. Its graduate overview does **not** list an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering; it lists an M.S. in Quantitative Systems Pharmacology instead. The state cohort therefore includes the BME Ph.D. and excludes an unverified BME M.S. claim.
