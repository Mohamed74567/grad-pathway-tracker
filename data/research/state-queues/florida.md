## University of Florida Biomedical Engineering refresh

UF’s official Biomedical Engineering admissions page establishes annual Fall guidance of December 5 for Ph.D. applications and January 5 priority/January 31 regular for master’s applications; because it does not name an admission cycle, these dates are retained as annual detail-page guidance rather than planner dates. It establishes the US$30 fee, no-GRE treatment, TOEFL/IELTS-only wording, shared M.S./M.E. pool, application materials, and Ph.D. supplemental application. The official funding page supports qualified funding availability for both degrees rather than a blanket guarantee: the majority of admitted Ph.D. applicants are offered fellowships and/or research assistantships, while M.S. support is limited to selected scholarship or faculty-supported cases. The refreshed profiles use a credited official UF Health Biomedical Sciences Building hero.

**Completed August 14, 2026:** the inherited University of Florida Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. and M.S. profiles now meet the six-source, three-pass, credited-image standard and were visually verified.

## University of South Florida Biomedical Engineering research findings

USF’s official Biomedical Engineering doctoral and master’s pages identify the degree paths, curriculum, admission materials, and currently published GRE treatment: GRE is required except for engineering graduates from ABET-accredited programs. The doctoral page lists a preferred 3.50 undergraduate GPA, related master’s degree, statement/CV, and three letters; the master’s page lists a 3.00 undergraduate GPA, engineering/science bachelor’s, two letters, statement, and CV/résumé. The master’s source names TOEFL wording for international students and defers to the Graduate Catalog for English-proficiency routes. Central graduate admissions establishes US$30 fee, official application access, test-score procedures, and final university deadlines while directing applicants to programs for any earlier priority date.
