## Joint UND–NDSU Biomedical Engineering M.S. findings — August 15, 2026

The official UND catalog confirms that the M.S. in Biomedical Engineering is jointly offered by UND’s College of Engineering and Mines, School of Medicine and Health Sciences, and NDSU’s College of Engineering. The degree has distinct 30-credit thesis and non-thesis options, with the non-thesis route requiring a final report. Admission requires an ABET-accredited engineering bachelor’s degree or provisional entry with added undergraduate engineering background, plus a 3.0 GPA (with provisional entry possible below 3.0). UND’s program page confirms on-campus or online format, medical-device and innovation focus, and the thesis/non-thesis distinction.

Central official evidence supports a US$35 nonrefundable fee, direct UND graduate application, three references, statement of goals, and qualified graduate-assistantship availability (departmental applications; fully admitted students with 3.0 GPA). The current central international graduate standards specify IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 4.0 on the post-January 2026 scale, or Duolingo 110, while also stating program-specific deadlines take precedence. The reviewed BME M.S. sources do not state a GRE requirement or a current program-specific deadline, so both remain blank unless independently verified. The official Biomedical Research and Labs page supplies BME research/infrastructure context and a potential official image source. **Ready for image staging and source-safe publication with no deadline record unless current program-specific timing is found.**

**NDSU reconciliation supplement:** NDSU’s current Biomedical Engineering master’s catalog confirms the same joint program and 30-credit Plan A thesis / Plan B master’s-paper structure. Its Graduate School application page states that, when a program catalog has no stated deadline, standard annual material deadlines are May 1 for Fall, October 1 for Spring, and March 1 for Summer; this BME catalog page does not state a different deadline. It also confirms US$35 fee, statement of purpose, CV, three recommendations, program-specific GRE treatment, TOEFL 71 / IELTS 6.0 / Duolingo 105 baseline standards, and department-based assistantship availability. These are recorded as central annual/default guidance, not as a BME-specific deadline promise. NDSU’s official joint-program page supplies a departmental image source.
