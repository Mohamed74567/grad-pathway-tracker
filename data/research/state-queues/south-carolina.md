## Clemson Bioengineering evidence

Clemson’s official Bioengineering graduate page confirms four separately awarded in-scope routes: research-oriented M.S. (thesis and non-thesis options), direct-entry 60-credit Ph.D., one-year non-thesis M.Eng., and an online M.S. in Medical Device Reprocessing. The program page supplies official department-hosted research images and Graduate Admissions application route. Doctoral research includes biomaterials, bioelectrical, biomechanics, regenerative medicine, and cellular/molecular technologies. A degree-specific current fee, funding, testing, and deadline pass remains required before publication.

## University of South Carolina Biomedical Engineering evidence

The University of South Carolina officially offers BME Ph.D., thesis M.S., and coursework-only M.E. degrees. The Ph.D. has 60 credits; the M.S. 30 credits plus research and thesis; the M.E. is a 30-credit professional degree. The degree page identifies optional GRE treatment and TOEFL 80/IELTS 6.5/PTE 53 language thresholds, but does not publish Duolingo treatment. Degree-specific funding, fee, deadline, direct application, and credited imagery evidence remains required before publication.
