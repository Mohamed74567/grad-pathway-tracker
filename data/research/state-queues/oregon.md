## OHSU Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. verification

OHSU’s official Biomedical Engineering program continues to identify a research-focused Ph.D. only. The current program overview states that full-time students receive a competitive monthly stipend and have all tuition and university fees covered. The curriculum page confirms a 135-credit doctorate with 54 dissertation-research credits, four electives, qualifying examination, and dissertation pathway. Its program-overview header provides official BME student laboratory imagery. Existing OHSU profile facts remain subject to source-safe refresh; no unsupported OHSU master’s record is eligible.

## Oregon State University–University of Oregon joint Bioengineering programs

Official Oregon State sources establish separately awarded joint M.S. and Ph.D. Bioengineering degrees with the University of Oregon, plus Oregon State’s coursework-oriented M.Eng. in Bioengineering. The joint M.S./Ph.D. applications have annual December 1 Fall guidance; the M.Eng. has annual May 1 Fall guidance. The official College page provides US$75 domestic/US$85 international application fees and directs applicants to OSU Graduate Admissions. The official Graduate School degree page states no GRE is required and gives TOEFL iBT 88/IELTS 7.0 treatment. Remaining passes must verify program-specific funding, Duolingo treatment, degree structure, direct routes, and credited imagery.

## University of Oregon joint-program clarification

The University of Oregon Knight Campus confirms that the Bioengineering Ph.D. is the same **dual-shield UO–OSU joint degree** already represented in this queue, with applications submitted to Oregon State’s Graduate School and reviewed by the joint admissions committee. It establishes the stronger Ph.D.-specific funding claim of a full tuition waiver and living stipend and confirms the annual December 1 full-consideration deadline, no-GRE rule, and a Duolingo English Test minimum of 120 for the joint application. The UO catalog says that an M.S. at the UO home campus is only attainable as part of the doctoral program, so no duplicate University of Oregon-only M.S. profile is created. The joint M.S. profile remains based on Oregon State’s explicit joint-M.S. publication and is distinguished from the UO home-campus pathway.

## Portland State eligibility determination

Portland State’s Biomedical Informatics listing is a selective five-year **B.S. Computer Science plus M.S. Biomedical Informatics accelerator** for high-achieving high-school applicants, with the graduate-year study completed through OHSU. PSU’s current engineering graduate-degree list presents it as “Computer Science B.S. + Biomedical Informatics M.S.” rather than an independently available graduate Bioengineering/BME/Neuroengineering degree. It is therefore outside this direct graduate-program directory cohort; no duplicate PSU profile is published.
