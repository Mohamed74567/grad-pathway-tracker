## UW–Madison Biomedical Engineering refresh findings

UW–Madison’s official Ph.D. page establishes 60 total credits, fall December 1 and spring September 1 program deadlines, no summer admission, no GRE requirement, three recommendations, statement of purpose, résumé, qualifying/preliminary/final-defense milestones, and funded doctoral treatment through research assistantships, teaching assistantships, and fellowships subject to funds and satisfactory progress.
