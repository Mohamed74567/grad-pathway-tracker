# University of South Florida Biomedical Engineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

USF’s current official Graduate Applicant Next Steps page states that all USF applicants must pay a nonrefundable US$30 application fee or request a fee waiver in cases of financial hardship. The same official page links a downloadable “Application Fee Waiver” form under common admission documents. It does not publish a BME-specific code, session, approval guarantee, or degree-level distinction.

The current USF BME search results identify separate published M.S. and Ph.D. Biomedical Engineering profiles. Because the route is stated for all USF graduate applicants and the linked form is on the official graduate-admissions page, it can be recorded separately on both degree profiles as a financial-hardship form route. The guidance must not imply automatic approval or transfer a tuition waiver into an application-fee waiver.

Sources: https://www.usf.edu/admissions/graduate/next-steps/check-application-status.aspx ; https://www.usf.edu/engineering/bme/graduate/graduateforms.aspx
