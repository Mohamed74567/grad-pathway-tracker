# University of Washington Bioengineering Ph.D. fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

UW Bioengineering’s current Ph.D. FAQ asks whether application fee waivers are offered and delegates the process to the UW Graduate School. The current Graduate School fee-waiver page lists limited waivers for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented applicants who demonstrate financial need or hardship under the Washington College Grant 100% Median Family Income threshold; Washington residency is not required and documentation may be requested for audit. Applicants expecting or holding F-1/J-1 status are not eligible for this financial-need waiver.

The Graduate School also lists McNair Scholars and PPIA Fellows as approved preparation-program categories, with supporting documentation requirements. Requests are made after submitting the application through the Applicant Status Portal; applicants must request at least seven days before the program deadline, and GEMS processes requests in received order. Applicants must not pay first because GEMS cannot waive or refund a paid fee. The UW Ph.D. admissions page states a US$90 application fee and the department FAQ provides no additional code or deadline.

This evidence supports a fee-waiver form/contact route for the published UW Bioengineering Ph.D. profile only. It does not establish a route for any separate master’s profile or a guaranteed departmental waiver.
