# Lehigh Bioengineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

The current official Lehigh BioE Graduate Admissions Guidelines identify both an M.S. in Bioengineering and a Ph.D. in Bioengineering. The shared application checklist requires a US$50 application fee payable to Lehigh University and states that it is non-refundable. The page publishes no application-fee waiver code, form, admission-session waiver, eligibility category, request contact, or timing exception for either degree. The page’s “waiver” references concern English-proficiency criteria, not application fees, and must not be misclassified.

The page says the Ph.D. deadline is December 15 each year and that December 15 is the priority deadline for maximum financial aid; those admissions/funding dates do not establish a fee waiver. No fee-waiver guidance should be added to the published Lehigh Bioengineering Ph.D. profile from this evidence, and no M.S. or Ph.D. cross-transfer is permitted.

Source: https://engineering.lehigh.edu/bioe/graduate/bioe-admissions-guidelines
