# Drexel Biomedical Engineering Fee-Waiver Working Notes

## Initial official review

Drexel Engineering’s current Admission Guidelines page states that no fee waivers are offered for its School of Engineering graduate programs, explicitly scoped to M.S., doctoral, and certificate programs. Drexel’s official Biomedical Engineering graduate-program page lists both full-time/part-time M.S. and Ph.D. offerings in Philadelphia, but its rendered content does not itself repeat the fee-waiver policy or state a program exception.

The next review must establish whether the Biomedical Engineering degree family is governed by the School of Engineering admission guidelines or whether its Biomedical Engineering & Science school supplies a distinct current application-fee policy. Until that is confirmed, no product guidance or no-waiver status will be changed.

Sources: https://drexel.edu/engineering/admissions/graduate/admission-guidelines/ ; https://drexel.edu/academics/grad-professional-programs/biomed/biomedical-engineering

## Reconciled boundary

The department-level **How to Apply** page is issued by the School of Biomedical Engineering, Science and Health Systems and provides one shared graduate checklist that links its M.S. and Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering to the Drexel application portal. It supplies no fee-waiver mechanism, eligibility category, contact, form, or event route. The official graduate-program directory confirms both M.S. and Ph.D. availability but likewise does not publish a waiver route. Although a separate School of Engineering page says no fee waivers are offered, the school identities are not identical in the current source set. The product therefore retains no fee-waiver guidance rather than transferring a no-waiver claim or a policy from another school.
