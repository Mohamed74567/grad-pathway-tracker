## Verified findings

Purdue BME’s current graduate admissions page explicitly says BME does not have authority to waive the application fee; the Office of the Vice Provost for Graduate Students and Postdoctoral Scholars (OGSPS) may waive it only in certain circumstances. The BME page applies to its administered Ph.D., thesis M.S., and professional M.S. options, and states a complete application includes payment unless an OGSPS waiver applies.

Purdue Engineering’s current Virtual Graduate Showcase fee-waiver page describes a concrete recruitment-event route. An applicant must attend the Purdue University Virtual Graduate Showcase, answer at least 10 questions in the Whova Resources > Survey section by Monday, October 12, 2026 at 11:59 PM EDT, and receive confirmation after October 13. In the OGSPS application, the applicant selects “Purdue Engineering Virtual Graduate Showcase” in the Recruitment Programs (Application Fee Waiver Eligibility) section. OGSPS verifies participation and emails approval information in approximately 2–3 days. The same email should be used for Whova registration and the application; manual confirmation can be requested at gradinfo@purdue.edu if needed. The waiver is for one application only, is non-refundable if the fee is paid, and does not automatically imply admission.

## Safe application wording

Add a `fee_waiver_session` entry to published Purdue Biomedical Engineering degree profiles that are covered by the West Lafayette BME admissions page. Link to the official Engineering Showcase fee-waiver instructions and state the exact survey deadline and redemption workflow. Preserve the BME department’s boundary that only OGSPS can waive the fee. Do not claim that every showcase attendee qualifies without completing the survey and verification. Do not transfer tuition-remission or assistantship benefits into application-fee guidance.

## Degree boundary

The showcase page is Engineering-wide and Purdue BME’s admissions page says OGSPS handles eligible waivers for BME Ph.D. and M.S. options. Apply the same event route separately to each published Purdue BME degree profile only; do not extend it to unrelated Purdue programs or regional campuses without a matching source.
