# UT Dallas Bioengineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

The current official UT Dallas Department of Bioengineering graduate page lists MS and PhD Biomedical Engineering programs and answers “Do you offer application fee waivers?” with: “No, we currently do not offer application fee waivers.”

The central Graduate Admissions pages separately describe a university-wide program for qualified participants in listed programs. It can cover PhD or master’s applicants who are U.S. citizens or permanent residents and provide documentation; the deadlines-and-fees page also lists UT Dallas students/alumni and current U.S. military eligibility, with documentation submitted through the Applicant Portal.

Because the BME department’s current program-level answer directly says it does not offer fee waivers, the central categories were not transferred to the published BME Ph.D. profile. This preserves the stronger degree/program-specific boundary and avoids implying that a central category has been confirmed for BME. No guidance row is added.
