# Washington University in St. Louis Biomedical Engineering fee-waiver review — August 26, 2026

The current official McKelvey School of Engineering Application Checklist states that the US$75 application-fee waiver is applied automatically for applicants who are current Washington University in St. Louis students or employees or who attend an eligible information session. The current official recruitment schedule says applicants are eligible for a waiver if they attend an information session, but not the Admissions Cram Session. The page currently lists a Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. admissions cram session on December 1, 2026 and explicitly says no waivers will be provided at that session; the BME master’s sessions listed are January 13, 2026 and therefore closed for the current date. A current BME-specific Fall 2027 master’s session was not published in the reviewed schedule.

The official BME Ph.D. page links the McKelvey application and has no contrary degree restriction. The official BME M.S. page describes the M.S. program but does not itself publish a current cycle-specific waiver date. Therefore, the shared engineering policy supports a general eligible-event/current-student route for both degree profiles, but any session date shown in guidance must be cycle-bounded and must explicitly exclude the Admissions Cram Session. Do not imply that attending a listed closed 2026 master’s session creates a current Fall 2027 waiver.
