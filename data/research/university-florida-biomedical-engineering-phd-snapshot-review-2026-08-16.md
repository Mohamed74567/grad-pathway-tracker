# University of Florida Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. snapshot review

**Review date:** 2026-08-16  
**Status:** Current-source findings captured; database reconciliation pending.

## Official sources reviewed

| Source | URL | Relevant current finding |
|---|---|---|
| UF BME Graduate Admissions | https://bme.ufl.edu/admissions/graduate-admissions/ | BME states GRE is not required; current nonrefundable fee is US$30. It lists Ph.D. Fall admission deadline as December 5 and says TOEFL or IELTS is required for applicable international applicants. |
| UF BME Graduate FAQs | https://bme.ufl.edu/academics/bme-graduate-program/graduate-faqs/ | Most admitted Ph.D. students are offered fellowships and/or research assistantships with stipend, tuition waiver, and health insurance. It describes full Ph.D. funding as typical, not an unconditional universal promise. |
| Herbert Wertheim College of Engineering Ph.D. prospects | https://www.eng.ufl.edu/graduate/prospects/ph-d-prospects/ | Current Fall priority deadline is December 5, and applications later than that may have reduced funding consideration. It repeats US$30 fee, GRE waiver, and TOEFL/IELTS treatment. |
| UF International Applicants | https://admissions.ufl.edu/apply/graduate/international | Current central policy lists TOEFL and IELTS requirements; it does not name Duolingo. |

## Source-safety implications

Retain or add BME’s current no-GRE policy, US$30 fee, and annual December 5 priority guidance, clearly scoped as annual guidance. English treatment is TOEFL/IELTS only; no Duolingo claim should be added. Doctoral funding should remain qualified as available rather than guaranteed because the FAQ says a majority/most Ph.D. admits receive support, even though packages can include stipend, tuition waiver, and health insurance.
