##  Application Process/Requirements 

###  \+ How do I apply? 

Apply online through the [University of Minnesota Graduate Application portal](https://choose.umn.edu/apply/).

In order to get started, you will need to create an online application. This will be used to submit all required documentation to apply to any of TLI's three degree programs, which are a part of the [College of Science and Engineering](https://cse.umn.edu/) at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities campus in Minneapolis. Visit the TLI ['How to Apply'](https://cse.umn.edu/tli/how-apply) page for a walk through each section of the application.

###  \+ When is the application deadline? 

  * Priority application deadline for admissions and scholarship consideration is January 15.
  * After January 15, we offer rolling admissions. Complete applications submitted during rolling admissions will receive a decision within six weeks of submission.
  * Last day to apply to any of our programs is July 15

###  \+ What are the guidelines for letters of recommendation? 

  * Letter writers should be professors, supervisors, or clients who have reviewed your academic and/or professional capabilities.
  * Enter the names and email addresses of your letter writers on the application
  * Your recommendation providers will receive an email from "[[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)" that contains a secure link to the recommendation portal for this application. The subject line will include your name and the words "Recommendation for the University of Minnesota." Please ask your recommendation providers to include this email address in their contact lists and to check spam/junk mail folders if they don't receive the message.
  * Request letters of recommendation early in the application process to allow enough time for your letter writers. Unsubmitted letters of recommendation are one of the most common delays during the application review process.
  * You may finish and submit your application before your letters are submitted by the writers



###  \+ What are the guidelines for the personal statement? 

A personal statement is generally 1-3 pages and should include the following:

  * Your interest in the graduate program and why you selected it.
  * Your managerial and/or technical leadership experience, abilities and interest in increasing your skills and opportunities.
  * Your expectations for program outcomes and how you anticipate using the skills you will gain to achieve your short and long term career goals.
  * Specific courses that you feel will apply to your current and future work challenges and needs.



###  \+ What are the guidelines/requirements for unofficial transcripts? 

  * If you have attended more than one post-secondary institution, please include all unofficial transcripts.
  * Upload an English translation if the transcript is not in English.



Please follow instructions on the [UMN Unofficial Transcripts & Credentials](https://grad.umn.edu/admissions/application-instructions/unofficial-transcripts-credentials) page.

###  \+ When are official transcripts required? 

Official transcripts/academic records are required after you are admitted to a graduate program. You will receive a list of the specific credentials that are required from you at the time of admission. Official transcripts must be sent directly to the University of Minnesota from the issuing institution. Do not send official transcripts to yourself first.

Additional information on official transcripts can be found via the [Graduate School](https://grad.umn.edu/admissions/application-instructions/official-transcripts-credentials).

##  Program Logistics 

###  \+ Can I start the program during the spring or summer semester? 

All of TLI’s programs start during the fall semester only.

###  \+ Can I enroll in the program part time? 

  * Because of our cohort model, we do not offer part time enrollment for our Master’s programs.
  * TLI’s programs are designed for working professionals so students can complete the program while working full time.



###  \+ Does the curriculum include project based classes? 

Yes, there are projects and team/group based activities throughout the curriculum.

###  \+ What format are the classes offered in? 

  * Courses may be offered in person or online, depending on the course.
  * Courses offered in person provide a zoom link so students can attend remotely if needed
  * Courses are recorded in case students are unable to attend



###  \+ When are courses offered? 

  * MOT courses happen on alternating Fridays and Saturdays from 8:00 am - 5:00 pm
  * ST and MDI courses typically happen from 5:30-9:30 pm 2-3 times per week
  * MDI students complete a Biopracticum during the final three semesters of the program. The Biopracticum course takes place all day Saturday, once a month.



###  \+ What is the work/life balance like for students working full time? 

  * TLI’s programs are designed for working professionals.
  * In our master’s programs, students typically enroll in 4-5 classes per semester. To help make the workload more manageable and improve work/life balance, generally, only 2-3 classes run concurrently. Some courses start at the beginning of the semester and conclude by mid-semester, while others begin mid-semester and finish at the end of the semester. There is limited overlap between the courses starting at the beginning and those starting mid-semester.
  * Courses for the MDI and ST programs take place in the evenings a couple times a week.
  * Courses for the MOT program take place alternating all-day Friday and Saturdays.
  * The coursework tends to be so relevant to our student's career that many people are able to apply what they're learning directly to ongoing projects at work and vice versa.



##  Finances/Funding 

###  \+ What is the general cost of the program? 

Visit our [tuition, fees, scholarships and financial aid page](https://cse.umn.edu/tli/tuition-fees-scholarships-and-financial-aid) for more information on the cost of the program.

###  \+ Do you provide funding to incoming students? 

  * As a department, we are unable to provide full funding for any of our students.
  * TLI does not offer teaching assistantships, graduate assistantships, research assistantships, or fellowships.
  * Most TLI students cover tuition through loans, personal funds, workplace tuition reimbursement benefits, military benefits, internships, and student employment
  * The Technological Leadership Institute offers a limited number of merit-based scholarships to incoming students each year across our master’s programs. Scholarship recipients receive a total award of $5,000, which is distributed evenly across the four semesters of the program. All applicants are automatically considered for scholarship funding—no separate application is required. Priority consideration is given to those who apply by January 15.



###  \+ Do you offer scholarships? 

  * Scholarships for incoming students
    * TLI has a limited number of merit-based scholarships we offer to a small group of incoming students across all three Master’s programs.
    * Scholarship awards are $5,000.
    * TLI automatically considers applicants for these scholarships based on their program application. Therefore, no special application is required for these scholarships.
    * Applicants who submit their applications by January 15 are given priority consideration for these funds. Applications received after the priority deadline will still be considered for scholarships from TLI, however, those applications submitted by the priority deadline will be given first consideration.
    * If an applicant receives a scholarship, they will be notified via their acceptance letter.
    * These scholarships are disturbed each semester, over the course of the two year program unless recipients request otherwise.
    * International students are eligible for these merit scholarships
