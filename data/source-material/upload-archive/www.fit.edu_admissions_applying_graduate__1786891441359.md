## Graduate Admission

Florida Tech welcomes applications for graduate study from students who wish to become experts and leaders in their field of study. Admission is granted to qualified applicants who demonstrate suitable preparation for advanced coursework and professional-level research. Graduate admission requirements may vary depending on program and degree type.

### Academic Preparedness

The following are minimum university requirements for admission to Florida Tech graduate programs. Some academic units have higher minimum standards for graduate admission.

Students looking to enroll in a master's degree program at Florida Tech must hold a bachelor's degree (four-year degree) in a related field from a recognized, accredited college or university (or the international equivalent).

Applicants must have earned a cumulative GPA of 3.0 as an undergraduate in order to be considered for admission to a Florida Tech master's degree program. Transcripts from a non-US institution are internally recalculated to meet this threshold.

If a student demonstrates acceptable undergraduate achievement but has subject-specific course deficiencies, the academic department that oversees the program will specify the criteria that must be met to make up for them.

Students who wish to pursue a doctoral degree at Florida Tech typically hold both a bachelor's and master's degree from a regionally accredited college or university.

Applicants must have earned a cumulative GPA of 3.2 in prior graduate coursework in order to be considered for admission to a Florida Tech doctoral degree program.

## Graduate Application Requirements

Prospective graduate students who are ready to apply may do so at any time, though some academic departments have established program-specific application deadlines that applicants should be aware of. Applications received after the program-specific deadline will be considered, but late applicants may be at a disadvantage with regards to admission, scholarship and assistantship consideration.

To apply, first complete the [application for graduate admission](https://admissions.fit.edu/apply/). Depending upon the program and degree type you have selected, you may be asked to submit some or all of the following materials. It is the applicant's responsibility to provide a complete application packet in order to be evaluated for admission. Please review the [Graduate Admission Policy](https://www.fit.edu/policies/graduate-policies-and-supporting-information/graduate-admission-/) for more information. 

#### Application Fee

A nonrefundable application fee of $60 (Master's), $80 (Doctoral), or $20 (Graduate Certificate Program) must accompany your application.

#### Transcripts

##### On-Campus Program Applicants

An official transcript from each college or university where 12 or more credits were earned must be submitted to the Office of Graduate Admission. Official transcripts must be sent directly by the issuing institution or registrar.

Applicants may complete a preliminary application review using unofficial transcripts; however, all outstanding official transcripts must be received before registration for the second semester. Refer to Methods for Submitting Official Transcripts below.

##### Online Program Applicants

Applicants to fully online programs must submit all official transcripts from institutions where 12 or more credits were earned before an application can be reviewed.

Official transcripts must be sent directly from the issuing institution. Refer to Methods for Submitting Official Transcripts below.

Unofficial transcripts are not accepted for application review for online programs.

##### International Applicants

Foreign Credential Evaluations & English Translations

Applicants with prior university-level education completed outside the United States must provide English translations of all application materials, including transcripts.

International students who are unable to submit official transcripts electronically may hand-deliver official transcripts upon arrival to campus.

Applicants who cannot obtain official transcripts from their international institution, or who require English translations, may use a [NACES-approved credential evaluation agency](https://naces.org/) and/or certified translation service.

Florida Tech partners with The Evaluation Company and Atlas Credential Evaluations to provide discounted rates on:

  * Certified translation services
  * Course-by-course foreign credential evaluations



Once completed, evaluation documents are automatically uploaded to the Florida Tech application portal. In some cases, foreign credential evaluations may satisfy the official final transcript requirement.

#### Recommendations

Some academic departments require a recommendation as part of the graduate application. A recommendation should speak to the applicant's prior academic and professional performance, as well as their potential for success in graduate study. Generally, recommendations are requested of advisors, mentors, professors or supervisors.

When recommendations are required, doctoral degree applicants should seek at least one from a full-time faculty member. Master's degree applicants are advised to request a recommendation from their master's thesis or final project advisor.

Recommendations may not be submitted by the student. Recommendations must come through the link provided to the recommender or directly to Graduate Admissions (counselor or [grad-admission@fit.edu](mailto:grad-admission@fit.edu) )

#### Résumé

The résumé you submit as part of your application for graduate admission should detail your academic credentials, prior employment (including teaching experience, if applicable) and scholarly achievements including but not limited to authorship/publications, conference participation and memberships in professional organizations. Applicants are encouraged to also include descriptions of relevant non-traditional educational and professional experiences.

#### Statement of Objectives

Departments that request a Statement of Objectives want to understand an applicant’s character, strengths, and aspirations—particularly how these relate to advanced study and research within their chosen field. A strong Statement of Objectives should be clear and concise (300-1000 words.) and describe the applicant’s academic interests, career goals, and both past and proposed scholarly activities. Please note that any program-specific requirements (please review below in Program-Specific Application Requirements for your program of interest) take precedence over the general Statement of Objectives guidelines.

#### Graduate Record Examination (GRE)

The [GRE](http://www.ets.org/gre/) is used to measure undergraduates' readiness for graduate study. Please reference the “Program Specific Application requirements” table below to verify GRE requirement for each graduate program.

In most cases, the GRE is a computer-based test, administered through designated sites located around the world. Testing locations in countries where technology may be limited often continue to administer paper tests. Students who take the computer-based GRE receive unofficial test results immediately after completing the examination and official results within 10-15 days. Students who take the paper GRE receive results four to six weeks after the examination date.

Students applying for an admission to a program that requires a GRE score are advised to plan accordingly and be sure to include Florida Institute of Technology (5080) as an official GRE score recipient.

#### Graduate Management Admissions Test (GMAT)

Although submitting [GMAT](https://www.mba.com/us) scores is not a requirement of admission to any of Florida Tech's graduate programs, it is a recommendation for students applying for entry into any of the university's graduate business programs, such as the [MBA](https://www.fit.edu/programs/business-administration-mba/) or [M.S. in Accounting & Financial Forensics](https://www.fit.edu/programs/accounting-and-financial-forensics-ms/). As an alternative, business program applicants may also submit GRE scores.

#### English Proficiency Requirements

While applicants whose home language is not English may be accepted into any degree program, these students will not be permitted to enroll in academic courses until certain [English proficiency requirements](/admission/admitted-students/undergraduate-international-student-next-steps/demonstrating-english-proficiency/) are met.

#### Demonstrating English Proficiency

Students may apply and be admitted to the university prior to the official submission of proof of English proficiency. Self-reported scores on the application are not considered. However, proof of English language proficiency must be submitted before the issuance of immigration documents or enrollment. This requirement applies to all modalities of degree and certificate programs.

#### Student Visa Requirements

Most international students must obtain an F-1 (student) visa, unless they already hold an immigration status that permits study in the United States. The first step in the process is to receive an I-20 from Florida Tech. Please refer to the [New Student Visa Requirements](https://www.fit.edu/admission/admitted-students/international-student-next-steps/new-student-visa-requirements/) webpage for details.

Florida Tech will not issue an I-20 until the following conditions have been met:

  * Students must be admitted to Florida Tech
  * Students must accept their offer of admission by paying the $300 tuition deposit
  * Students must demonstrate [English Language Proficiency](https://www.fit.edu/admissions/admitted-students/international-student-next-steps/demonstrating-english-proficiency/)(if English is not their first language)
  * Students must submit the required identification and financial documents (see [New Student Visa Requirements](https://www.fit.edu/admission/admitted-students/international-student-next-steps/new-student-visa-requirements/))



*Canadian and Bermudan students only require an I-20; they are not required to apply for an F-1 visa.
